Dwarvish is a weighty, deliberate language built on firm consonants and measured rhythm. Spoken well, it sounds carved rather than breathed, with each word given a sense of permanence. Its script is angular and durable, designed to be chiselled into stone, stamped into metal, or scratched clearly into tools and clan records.

***Typical Speakers.*** Dwarves
***Script.*** Dwarvish
