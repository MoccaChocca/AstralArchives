Deep Speech is alien and unsettling, marked by strange pauses, wet consonants, clicks, pressure-like sounds, and patterns that can feel almost telepathic. It is poorly suited to ordinary mortal mouths, so humanoid speakers often produce only an approximation. Since it has no common script, written Deep Speech is usually rendered through borrowed symbols, diagrams, or maddening personal notation.

***Typical Speakers.*** Aboleths, cloakers
