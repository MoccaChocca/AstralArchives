Halfling is warm, nimble, and conversational, rich in turns of phrase for family, food, travel, weather, bargains, and local gossip. Its softer sound makes it easy to speak quietly without losing meaning. Written Halfling uses Common script, often appearing in recipes, journals, family trees, travel notes, and friendly letters.

***Typical Speakers.*** Halflings
***Script.*** Common
