Goblin is sharp, clipped, and practical, with a strong taste for commands, insults, warnings, and jokes that land quickly. It changes easily between tribes, warbands, and cities, picking up slang wherever goblinoids survive. Written Goblin uses Dwarvish script in a rougher hand, often scratched, painted, or branded onto whatever surface is available.

***Typical Speakers.*** Goblinoids
***Script.*** Dwarvish
