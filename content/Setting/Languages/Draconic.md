Draconic is ancient, commanding, and sibilant, shaped by breath, scale, and the pride of dragons. It carries powerfully in open air and lends itself to names, oaths, threats, and formal declarations. Its script is composed of strong runic forms, often etched into stone, bone, metal, or treasure with a sense of age and authority.

***Typical Speakers.*** Dragons, dragonborn
***Script.*** Draconic
