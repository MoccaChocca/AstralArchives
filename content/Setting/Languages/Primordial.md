Primordial is an ancient elemental language expressed through four major dialects: Auran, Aquan, Ignan, and Terran. Its sound changes dramatically by dialect, from breath and rush, to fluid resonance, to crackling heat, to grinding stone. When written, Primordial commonly uses Dwarvish script, adapted to capture forces of air, water, fire, and earth in a mortal hand.

***Typical Speakers.*** Elementals
***Script.*** Dwarvish
