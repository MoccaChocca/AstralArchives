Common is the practical tongue of roads, ports, courts, and market squares. It tends to absorb useful words from neighbouring cultures, so its sound can shift from one region to the next while remaining broadly understood. Written Common favours clear, efficient characters suited to signs, ledgers, letters, and everyday record keeping.

***Typical Speakers.*** Humans
***Script.*** Common
