Celestial is clear, resonant, and solemn, with a musical quality that can make even simple statements sound ceremonial. It favours precision, truth, and layered symbolism, making it well suited to vows, blessings, prophecies, and sacred law. Its script is balanced and luminous in style, often written with careful spacing and elegant symmetry.

***Typical Speakers.*** Celestials
