Orc is direct, forceful, and rhythmic, favouring strong verbs and clear declarations over delicate phrasing. In speech it can sound harsh to outsiders, but skilled speakers use cadence, pause, and repetition to carry honour, grief, anger, and respect. Its Dwarvish-derived script is usually written in bold, heavy marks suited to banners, weapons, stone, and hide.

***Typical Speakers.*** Orcs
***Script.*** Dwarvish
