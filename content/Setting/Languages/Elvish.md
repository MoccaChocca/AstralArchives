Elvish is fluid and graceful, favouring long vowels, subtle inflection, and phrasing that can carry several shades of meaning at once. Its written form is elegant and flowing, often arranged with an eye for beauty as much as clarity. Letters, songs, oaths, and histories written in Elvish can feel more like art than plain record.

***Typical Speakers.*** Elves
***Script.*** Elvish
