Druidic is a guarded language of primal circles, old groves, standing stones, and sacred oaths. Spoken aloud, it is often low, rhythmic, and naturalistic, with tones that echo birdsong, rustling leaves, running water, and distant thunder. In writing, Druidic uses Elvish script, but its meaning is often veiled through seasonal marks, symbolic spacing, and signs worked into bark, stone, bone, and woven cord. To outsiders, it may look like decoration or wilderness craft; to those who know it, it carries law, warning, memory, and reverence.

***Typical Speakers.*** Druids
***Script.*** Elvish
