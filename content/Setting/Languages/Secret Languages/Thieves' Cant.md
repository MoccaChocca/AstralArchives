Thieves' Cant is less a single spoken tongue than a layered code of slang, implication, signs, and shared tricks. It can hide meaning inside ordinary conversation, turning a casual remark about weather, trade, or directions into a warning, bargain, threat, or invitation. Written Thieves' Cant uses Common script, but its message is often concealed in marks on doorframes, altered punctuation, false account books, folded notes, chalk symbols, or deliberate mistakes. To the uninitiated it reads as nothing unusual; to a trained eye, it maps debts, dangers, safehouses, and opportunities.

***Typical Speakers.*** Thieves, rogues, criminals, spies, and members of hidden underworld networks
***Script.*** Common
