Sylvan is lyrical, playful, and slippery, rich with double meanings, emotional turns, and expressions that depend on mood, season, and intent. It can sound beautiful, mocking, mournful, or dangerous with only the smallest shift in tone. Written in Elvish script, Sylvan often curls into decorative forms that suit songs, riddles, invitations, bargains, and warnings left in wild places.

***Typical Speakers.*** Fey creatures
***Script.*** Elvish
