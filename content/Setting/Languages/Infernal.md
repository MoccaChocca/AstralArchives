Infernal is precise, severe, and deliberate, built for command, hierarchy, bargains, and binding terms. Its beauty is cold rather than gentle, with grammar that rewards exact wording and punishes careless implication. Written Infernal is dense and disciplined, making it ideal for contracts, seals, laws, and infernal decrees.

***Typical Speakers.*** Devils
***Script.*** Infernal
