Abyssal is violent, unstable, and unpleasant to hear, full of guttural sounds, abrupt changes in rhythm, and snarled emphasis. It often feels less like a language built for conversation and more like one torn from rage, hunger, and command. When written in Infernal script, Abyssal tends to distort the orderly letters into jagged, crowded forms that seem to resist neat arrangement.

***Typical Speakers.*** Demons
***Script.*** Infernal
