Undercommon is a hard-working trade language of the deep places, shaped by merchants, exiles, scouts, prisoners, and underworld powers that need to bargain without trusting one another. It is practical, cautious, and filled with loanwords from subterranean cultures. Written in Elvish script, it is often abbreviated, coded, or marked for secrecy on stone, fungus paper, trade slates, and hidden signs.

***Typical Speakers.*** Underworld traders
***Script.*** Elvish
