Giant is broad, thunderous, and physical, with sounds that favour deep breath, open vowels, and forceful emphasis. It is a language suited to halls, mountainsides, and battlefields rather than quiet rooms. When written, Giant commonly uses Dwarvish script, though its letters are often enlarged, simplified, and cut with brutal confidence.

***Typical Speakers.*** Ogres, Giants
***Script.*** Dwarvish
