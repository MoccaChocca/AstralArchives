Dwarves are sturdy folk shaped by stone halls, ancient craft, and enduring traditions. Whether raised in mountain citadels or among other peoples, they are famed for resilience, long memory, and a deep bond with the earth beneath their feet.

### Dwarf Traits

***Creature Type:*** Humanoid

***Size:*** Medium

***Speed:*** 30 feet

As a Dwarf, you have these special traits.

**Darkvision.** You have [[Special Senses|Darkvision]] with a range of 120 feet.

**Dwarven Resilience.** You have [[Damage Resistance and Vulnerability|Resistance]] to Poison damage. You also have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on [[saving throws]] you make to avoid or end the [[Poisoned]] condition.

**Dwarven Toughness.** Your [[Hit Points|Hit Point maximum]] increases by 1, and it increases by 1 again whenever you gain a level.

**Stonecunning.** As a [[Bonus Action]], you gain [[Special Senses|Tremorsense]] with a range of 60 feet for 10 minutes. You must be on a stone surface or touching a stone surface to use this [[Special Senses|Tremorsense]]. The stone can be natural or worked.

You can use this [[Bonus Action]] a number of times equal to your [[Proficiency Bonus]], and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [[Resting|Long Rest]].
