Desc: Knock a creature Prone with a weapon or Unarmed Strike
---
When you hit a creature with an [[Attack Rolls|attack roll]] using a weapon or an Unarmed Strike, you can expend one Superiority Die and add the die to the attack’s damage roll. If the target is Large or smaller, it must succeed on a [[Strength]] [[Saving Throws|saving throw]] or have the [[Prone]] condition.
