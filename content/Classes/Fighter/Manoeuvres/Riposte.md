Desc: Strike back when a creature misses you in melee
---
When a creature misses you with a melee [[Attack Rolls|attack roll]], you can take a [[Reaction]] and expend one Superiority Die to make a melee [[Attack Rolls|attack roll]] with a weapon or an Unarmed Strike against the creature. If you hit, add the Superiority Die to the attack’s damage.
