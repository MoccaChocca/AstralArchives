Desc: Swap places with an ally and grant a temporary bonus to AC
---
When you’re within 5 feet of a creature on your turn, you can expend one Superiority Die and switch places with that creature, provided you spend at least 5 feet of [[Rules/Combat/Movement/Movement|movement]] and the creature is willing and doesn’t have the [[Incapacitated]] condition. This [[Rules/Combat/Movement/Movement|movement]] doesn’t provoke [[Opportunity Attacks]].

Roll the Superiority Die. Until the start of your next turn, you or the other creature (your choice) gains a bonus to AC equal to the number rolled.
