Desc: Carry your strike through to damage a second creature
---
When you hit a creature with a melee [[Attack Rolls|attack roll]] using a weapon or an Unarmed Strike, you can expend one Superiority Die to attempt to damage another creature. 

Choose another creature within 5 feet of the original target and within your reach. If the original [[Attack Rolls|attack roll]] would hit the second creature, it takes damage equal to the number you roll on your Superiority Die. The damage is of the same type dealt by the original attack.
