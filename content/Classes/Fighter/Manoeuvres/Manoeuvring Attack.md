Desc: Strike an enemy and move an ally into a better position
---
When you hit a creature with an [[Attack Rolls|attack roll]], you can expend one Superiority Die to manoeuvre one of your comrades into another position. Add the Superiority Die roll to the attack’s damage roll, and choose a willing creature who can see or hear you. That creature can use its [[Reaction]] to move up to half its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks from the target of your attack.
