Desc: Direct an ally to attack using their Reaction
---
When you take the [[Attack action]] on your turn, you can replace one of your attacks to direct one of your companions to strike. 

When you do so, choose a willing creature who can see or hear you and expend one Superiority Die. That creature can immediately use its [[Reaction]] to make one attack with a weapon or an Unarmed Strike, adding the Superiority Die to the attack’s damage roll on a hit.
