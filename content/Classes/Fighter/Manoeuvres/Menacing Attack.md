Desc: Make a ferocious strike against your opponent, attempting to frighten them
---
When you hit a creature with an [[Attack Rolls|attack roll]], you can expend one Superiority Die to attempt to frighten the target. Add the Superiority Die to the attack’s damage roll.

The target must succeed on a [[Wisdom]] [[Saving Throws|saving throw]] or have the [[Frightened]] condition until the end of your next turn.
