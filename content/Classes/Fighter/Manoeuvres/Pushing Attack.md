Desc: Drive your target backwards with a powerful attack
---
When you hit a creature with an [[Attack Rolls|attack roll]] using a weapon or an Unarmed Strike, you can expend one Superiority Die to attempt to drive the target back. Add the Superiority Die to the attack’s damage roll. 

If the target is Large or smaller, it must succeed on a [[Strength]] [[Saving Throws|saving throw]] or be pushed up to 15 feet directly away from you.
