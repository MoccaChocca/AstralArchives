Desc: Dash forward and add your Superiority Die to a melee attack
---
As a [[Bonus Action]], you can expend one Superiority Die and take the [[Dash|Dash action]]. If you move at least 5 feet in a straight line immediately before hitting with a melee attack as part of the [[Attack action]] on this turn, you can add the Superiority Die to the attack’s damage roll.
