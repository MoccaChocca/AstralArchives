Desc: Goad your opponent into focusing their attacks on you
---
When you hit a creature with an [[Attack Rolls|attack roll]], you can expend one Superiority Die to attempt to goad the target into attacking you. Add the Superiority Die to the attack’s damage roll. The target must succeed on a [[Wisdom]] [[Saving Throws|saving throw]] or have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on [[Attack Rolls|attack rolls]] against targets other than you until the end of your next turn.
