Desc: Reduce the damage from a melee attack against you
---
When another creature damages you with a melee [[Attack Rolls|attack roll]], you can take a [[Reaction]] and expend one Superiority Die to reduce the damage by the number you roll on your Superiority Die plus your [[Strength]] or [[Dexterity]] modifier (your choice).
