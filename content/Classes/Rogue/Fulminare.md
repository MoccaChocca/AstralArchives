## **Level 3: Instruments of Destruction**

You gain [[Expertise]] with Alchemist’s and Potter’s tools. Additionally, alchemical items you throw that require an [[Attack Rolls|attack roll]] may benefit from your sneak attack feature.

## **Level 3: Cunning Tricks**

Your fell tricks allow you to gain the upper hand. You learn the following tricks, which you use with the [[bonus action]] granted to you by Cunning Action.

***Smokesmother.*** You create a plume of smoke that smothers flame and conceals you. This functions as the [[Fog Cloud]] spell except it targets your space, has a radius of 15ft and lasts a minute. Additionally, any non-magical flame is extinguished and any creature stops Burning. A creature burning in this way takes 1d10 damage instead.

***Tripburn.*** You lob a vial that sprays out slick, flammable oil up to 30ft away. You create a puddle of oil as per the [[Grease]] spell. A creature that falls [[prone]] in this area becomes greased. The next time a greased creature takes fire or lightning damage it starts Burning.

***Flashblind.*** You use a blinding flash to escape enemies. You throw an explosive at the feet of an enemy within 30ft. The enemy must make a [[Constitution]] [[Saving Throws|Saving throw]] or be [[Blinded]] until the start of your next turn. Any creature within 5ft of the enemy may move up to half their speed without provoking reactions from the target creature.

***Grabgoop.*** You throw out a vial of expanding sticky and slick foam at a space within 30ft. Spaces within a 15ft [[Areas of Effect|cube]] become [[difficult terrain]] for the next minute. Creatures within a 15ft [[Areas of Effect|cube]] must make a [[Strength]] [[Saving Throws|Saving Throw]] or become [[Restrained]] for 1 minute. 

Unless otherwise specified, a creature affected by your tricks as the result of a failed [[Saving Throws|saving throw]],  may reattempt the save at their turn, ending it on a success. 

The DC for your Tricks is equal to 8 \+ your proficiency \+ your [[intelligence]] modifier. You may use these Tricks a number of times equal to your [[intelligence]] modifier \+ 1, regaining all uses on a [[Resting|long rest]].

## **Level 9: Explosive Additives**

You attach small explosive packages to your sneak attack’s strikes, laced with special additives. You learn the following Cunning Strike options.

***Nut Shells (Cost 1d6).*** A seismic shock breaks apart the nearby earth. Ground within 10ft of creatures taking your sneak attack’s damage becomes [[difficult terrain]].

***Quartz (Cost 2d6).*** A bright cacophony of colours forces creatures taking your sneak attack’s damage to make a [[Constitution]] [[Saving Throws|Saving Throw]] or be Dazzled.  The next time a Dazzled creature attacks, it provokes Attacks of Opportunity, it misses and it is no longer Dazzled.

***Glass Bead (Cost 3d6).*** You prime your strike with an explosive charge. Creatures within 10ft of your target must make a [[Dexterity]] [[Saving Throws|Saving Throw]] or take your sneak attack’s damage as well.

## **Level 13: Confounding Tricks**

Your fell tricks take on a confounding twist, making them more potent, deadly and useful. Your tricks gain the following benefits:

***Smokesmother.*** You supplement your allies with techniques and chemicals at the beginning of a day. You and creatures of your choice in the smoke can see through the smoke and gain [[Resistance]] to fire damage and [[Cover|half cover]] while in the smoke.

***Tripburn.*** When you throw this vial, any creature in the radius becomes greased without needing to fall [[prone]]. Additionally, a creature takes 2d10 fire damage while Burning.

***Flashblind.*** A creature that fails its [[Saving Throws|Saving Throw]] against this is also [[Stunned]] until the start of your next turn.

***Grabgoop.*** A creature that succeeds its [[Saving Throws|saving throw]] against this has its speed reduced by half. While a creature is [[restrained]] by this it takes 2d10 acid damage at the start of its turns.

## **Level 13: Spontaneous Combustion**

You always have the bits and bobs on-hand to whip up another trick. When you take a [[Resting|short rest]] or roll [[initiative]], you regain 1 use of your Cunning Tricks.

## **Level 17: Chemical Warfare** 

Your destructive foresight knows no bounds. When you deal sneak attack damage to a creature, you may expend one of your Cunning Tricks, no action required. When you do so, if that trick requires you to target a space it targets that creature's space, if it requires you to target a creature it targets that creature.

If that strike was a [[Critical Hits|critical hit]], any [[saving throws]] it makes against the Trick are made with [[Advantage and Disadvantage|disadvantage]].
