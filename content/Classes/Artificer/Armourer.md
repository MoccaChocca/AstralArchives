 *Craft Magic armour to Enhance Your Abilities*  
  
An Artificer who specializes as an armourer modifies armour to function almost like a second skin. The armour is enhanced to hone the Artificer’s magic, unleash potent attacks, and generate a formidable defence.
  
## Level 3: Tools of the Trade  
  
You gain training with Heavy armour. You also gain proficiency with Smith’s Tools. If you already have this tool proficiency, you gain proficiency with one other type of Artisan’s Tools of your choice.
  
In addition, when you craft nonmagical or magic armour, the amount of time required to craft it is halved.
  
## Level 3: armourer Spells  
  
When you reach an Artificer level specified in the armourer Spells table, you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

## Level 3: Arcane armour  
  
As a [[Magic action]] while you have Smith’s Tools in hand, you can turn a suit of armour you are wearing into Arcane armour. The armour continues to be Arcane armour until you don another suit of armour or you die.

You gain the following benefits while wearing your Arcane armour:

***No Strength Requirement.*** If the armour normally has a [[Strength]] requirement, the Arcane armour lacks this requirement for you.

***Quick Don and Doff.*** You can don or doff the armour as a [[Utilise|Utilise action]].

***Second Skin.*** The armour attaches to you and can’t be removed against your will. It also expands to cover your entire body, although you can retract or deploy the helmet as a [[Bonus Action]]. The armour replaces missing limbs, functioning identically to a limb it replaces.

***Spellcasting Focus.*** You can use the Arcane armour as a Spellcasting Focus for your Artificer spells.

## Level 3: armour Model  
You can customize your Arcane armour. When you do so, choose one of the following armour models: Dreadnaught, Guardian, or Infiltrator. The model you choose gives you special benefits while you wear it.

Each model includes a special weapon. When you attack with that weapon, you can add your [[Intelligence]] modifier, instead of [[Strength]] or [[Dexterity]] modifier, to the attack and damage rolls.

You can change the armour’s model whenever you finish a [[Resting|Short or Long Rest]] if you have Smith’s Tools in hand.

### Dreadnaught 
You design your armour to become a towering juggernaut in battle. It has the following features:

***Armour Flail.*** An iron ball on a chain appears on one of your armour’s gauntlets. The flail is a simple melee weapon that deals 1d10 Bludgeoning damage and has the Reach trait.

***Giant Stature.*** As a [[Bonus Action]], you transform and enlarge your armour for 1 minute. For the duration, your reach increases by 5 feet, and if you are smaller than Large, you become Large, along with anything you are wearing. If there isn’t enough room for you to increase your size, your size doesn’t change. You can use this [[Bonus Action]] a number of times equal to your [[Intelligence]] modifier (minimum of once). You regain all expended uses when you finish a [[Resting|Long Rest]].

***Wrecking Ball.*** If you hit a creature that is at least one size smaller than you with the flail, you can push the creature up to 10 feet straight away from yourself or pull the creature up to 10 feet toward yourself.

### Guardian
You design your armour to be in the front line of conflict. It has the following features:

***Thunder Gauntlets.*** Your gauntlets crackle and pulse with arcane energy. They are a simple melee weapon and deal 1d8 Thunder damage.

***Distracting Pulse.*** A creature hit by the gauntlet has [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on [[attack rolls]] against targets other than you until the start of your next turn.

***Defensive Field.*** While you are [[Bloodied]], you can take a [[Bonus Action]] to gain [[Temporary Hit Points]] equal to your Artificer level. You lose these [[Temporary Hit Points]] if you doff the armour.

### Infiltrator
You customize your armour for subtle undertakings. It has the following features:

***Lightning Launcher.*** A gemlike node appears on one of the armour’s gauntlets or on the armour’s chest (your choice). The launcher is a simple ranged weapon that deals 1d6 Lightning damage and has a range of 90/300 feet.

***Lightning Jolt.*** Once on each of your turns when you hit a creature with your Lightning Launcher, you can deal an extra 1d6 Lightning damage to that target.

***Powered Steps.*** Your Speed increases by 5 feet.

***Dampening Field.*** You have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on [[Dexterity|Dexterity (Stealth)]] checks. If the armour normally imposes [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on such checks, the [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] and [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] cancel each other, as normal.

## Level 5: Extra Attack  

You can attack twice instead of once whenever you take the [[Attack action]] on your turn.

## Level 9: armour Replication  
  
You learn an additional plan for your Replicate Magic Item feature, and it must be in the armour category. If you replace that plan, you must replace it with another armour plan.

In addition, you can create an additional item with your Replicate Magic Item feature, and the item must also be in the armour category.

## Level 15: Perfected armour  
  
Your Arcane armour gains additional benefits based on its model, as detailed below.
  
***Dreadnaught.*** The damage die of your armour Flail increases to 2d6 Bludgeoning damage.
  
In addition, when you use your Giant Stature, your reach increases by 10 feet, your size can increase to Large or Huge (your choice), and you gain a [[Rules/Core/Movement/Movement|Fly Speed]] equal to your Speed.
  
***Guardian.*** The damage die of your Thunder Gauntlets increases to 1d10 Thunder damage.
  
In addition, when a Huge or smaller creature you can see ends its turn within 30 feet of you, you can take a [[Reaction]] to magically force that creature to make a [[Saving Throws|Strength saving throw]] against your spell save DC. On a failed save, you pull the creature up to 25 feet directly to an unoccupied space. If you pull the target to a space within 5 feet of yourself, you can make a melee weapon attack against it as part of this [[Reaction]].
  
You can take this [[Reaction]] a number of times equal to your [[Intelligence]] modifier (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [[Resting|Long Rest]].
  
***Infiltrator.*** The damage die of your Lightning Launcher increases to 2d6. Any creature that takes Lightning damage from your Lightning Launcher glimmers with magical light until the start of your next turn. The glimmering creature sheds [[Vision|Dim Light]] in a 5-foot radius, and it has Disadvantage on [[attack rolls]] against you, as the light jolts it if it attacks you.
