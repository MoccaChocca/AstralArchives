*Metamagic Option (Cost: 1 Sorcery Point)*

When you cast a spell that forces other creatures to make a [[Saving Throws|saving throw]], you can protect some of those creatures from the spell’s full force. 

Spend 1 Sorcery Point and choose a number of those creatures up to your [[Charisma]] modifier (minimum of one creature). A chosen creature automatically succeeds on its [[Saving Throws|saving throw]] against the spell, and it takes no damage if it would normally take half damage on a successful save.
