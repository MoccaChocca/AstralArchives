*Metamagic Option (Cost: 1 Sorcery Point)*

When you cast a spell, you can spend 1 Sorcery Point to cast it without any Verbal, Somatic, or [[Components|Material components]], except [[Components|Material components]] that are consumed by the spell or that have a cost specified in the spell.
