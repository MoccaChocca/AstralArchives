*Metamagic Option (Cost: 2 Sorcery Points)*

When you cast a spell that forces a creature to make a [[Saving Throws|saving throw]], you can spend 2 Sorcery Points to give one target of the spell [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on saves against the spell.
