*Eldritch Invocation (Prerequisite: Level 5+ Warlock)*

You can breathe underwater, and you gain a Swim Speed equal to your Speed.

You can also cast *[[Water Breathing]]* once without expending a spell slot. You regain the ability to cast it in this way again when you finish a [[Resting|Long Rest]].
