*Eldritch Invocation (Prerequisite: Level 2+ Warlock, a Warlock Cantrip That Deals Damage via an Attack Roll)*

Choose one of your known Warlock cantrips that requires an [[Attack Rolls|attack roll]]. When you hit a Large or smaller creature with that cantrip, you can push the creature up to 10 feet straight away from you.

***Repeatable.*** You can gain this invocation more than once. Each time you do so, choose a different eligible cantrip.
