*Eldritch Invocation (Prerequisite: Level 9+ Warlock, [[Pact of the Blade]] Invocation)*

Once per turn when you hit a creature with your pact weapon, you can deal an extra 1d6 Necrotic, Psychic, or Radiant damage (your choice) to the creature, and you can expend one of your [[Hit Points|Hit Point]] Dice to roll it and regain a number of [[Hit Points]] equal to the roll plus your [[Constitution]] modifier (minimum of 1 [[Hit Points|Hit Point]]).
