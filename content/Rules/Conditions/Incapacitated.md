- An incapacitated creature can’t take actions, bonus actions, free actions, or reactions.
- An incapacitated creature's [[concentration]] is broken.
- An incapacitated creature can't speak.
- If the creature is Incapacitated when it rolls [[Initiative]], it has [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on the roll.
