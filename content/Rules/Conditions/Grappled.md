- A grappled creature’s Speed is 0 and can’t increase.  
- A grappled creature has [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on [[attack rolls]] against any target other than the grappler.  
- The grappler can [[Strength|drag]] or carry the grappled creature when it moves. Each foot of [[Rules/Combat/Movement/Movement|movement]] costs the grappler 1 extra foot, unless the grappled creature is at least two sizes smaller than the grappler.
