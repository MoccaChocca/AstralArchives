Some special abilities and environmental hazards, such as starvation and the long-term effects of freezing or scorching temperatures, can lead to exhaustion. 

If an already exhausted creature suffers another effect that causes exhaustion, its current level of exhaustion increases by the amount specified in the effect.  
  
An effect that removes exhaustion reduces its level as specified in the effect. A creature’s exhaustion level is reduced by 1 when it finishes a [[Resting|long rest]], provided it has also had some [[Food and Water|food and drink]].

- Exhaustion is cumulative. Each time a creature gains Exhaustion, its Exhaustion level increases by 1.  
- A creature dies if its Exhaustion level reaches 6.  
- Whenever an Exhausted creature makes a [[D20 Tests|D20 Test]], it subtracts 2 times its Exhaustion level from the roll.  
- An Exhausted creature’s Speed is reduced by 5 feet times its Exhaustion level.  
- When a creature’s Exhaustion level reaches 0, the condition ends.
