Combatants often find themselves lying on the ground, either because they are knocked down or because they throw themselves down. In the game, they are prone. You can drop prone without using any of your speed. Standing up takes more effort; doing so costs an amount of [[Rules/Combat/Movement/Movement|movement]] equal to half your speed. For example, if your speed is 30 feet, you must spend 15 feet of [[Rules/Combat/Movement/Movement|movement]] to stand up. You can't stand up if you don't have enough [[Rules/Core/Movement/Movement|movement]] left or if your speed is 0.

To move while prone, you must [[Rules/Core/Movement/Movement|crawl]] or use magic such as teleportation. Every foot of [[Rules/Combat/Movement/Movement|movement]] while [[Rules/Core/Movement/Movement|crawling]] costs 1 extra foot. [[Rules/Core/Movement/Movement|Crawling]] 1 foot in [[difficult terrain]], therefore, costs 3 feet of [[Rules/Combat/Movement/Movement|movement]].

- The creature has [[Advantage and Disadvantage|disadvantage]] on [[attack rolls]].  
- [[Attack rolls]] against the creature have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|advantage]] if the attacker is within 5 feet of the creature. Otherwise, [[attack rolls]] against the creature have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|disadvantage]].
