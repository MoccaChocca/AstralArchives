You are taking damage from an ongoing effect, such as from being lit on fire. This appears as “X persistent (type) damage,” where “X” is the amount of damage dealt and “(type)” is the damage type. Instead of taking persistent damage immediately, you take it at the end of each of your turns as long as you have the condition, rolling any damage dice anew each time.

The effect will specify when the Persistent Damage ends, such as the *[[Searing Smite]]* spell. You can be simultaneously affected by multiple persistent damage conditions.

#### Assisted Recovery
You or an ally can take steps to help recover from Persistent Damage. This is usually an activity that requires the [[Utilise|Utilise action]] or the [[Help|Help action]], and it must be something that would reasonably improve your chances (as determined by the DM). For example, you might try to smother a flame or wash off acid. This allows you to attempt a [[Saving Throws|Saving Throw]] against the effect immediately, but only once per round.

The DM decides how your help works, using the following examples as guidelines.  

- The action to help might require an [[Ability Checks|Ability Check]] to determine its effectiveness.
- Automatically end the condition due to the type of recovery, such as submerging yourself in a lake to end persistent fire damage.
- Alter the type of actions required to help you if the means the helper uses are especially efficient or remarkably inefficient.
