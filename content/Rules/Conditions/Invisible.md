An invisible creature is affected in the following ways, provided other creatures cannot otherwise see the invisible creature:

- If the creature is invisible when it rolls [[Initiative]], it has [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on the roll.  
- The creature cannot be affected by effects that require their target to be seen
- [[Attack rolls]] against the creature have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]].
- The creature’s [[attack rolls]] have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]].
