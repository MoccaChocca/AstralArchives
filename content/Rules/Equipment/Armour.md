Armor protects adventurers from the countless dangers of battle, turning aside blades, arrows, claws, and spells that might otherwise prove fatal. From a simple suit of padded leather to a gleaming coat of plate, armour represents preparation, training, and survival. The right armour can keep a warrior standing through the chaos of combat, but heavier protection often comes at the cost of speed, subtlety, and weight.

### Armor Training
Anyone can don armour or hold a Shield, but only those with training can use them effectively, as explained below. A character’s class and other features determine the character’s armour training. A monster has training with any armour in its stat block.

##### Light, Medium, or Heavy Armor
If you wear Light, Medium, or Heavy armour and lack training with it, you have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on any [[D20 Tests|D20 Test]] that involves [[Strength]] or [[Dexterity]], and you can't cast spells.

##### Shield
You gain the Armor Class benefit of a Shield only if you have training with it.

### One Set at a Time
A creature can wear only one suit of armour at a time and wield only one Shield at a time.

### Donning and Doffing Armor

Donning armour is the process of putting it on, while doffing armour is the process of taking it off. The time required depends on the armour’s category, as shown in the Donning and Doffing Armor table.

| Armor Category |              Don |             Doff |
| -------------- | ---------------: | ---------------: |
| Light Armor    |         1 minute |         1 minute |
| Medium Armor   |        5 minutes |         1 minute |
| Heavy Armor    |       10 minutes |        5 minutes |
| Shield         | 1 [[Utilise\|Utilise action]] | 1 [[Utilise\|Utilise action]] |

A creature gains the benefit of armour or a Shield only while it is properly worn or wielded. Armor can’t usually be put on or removed quickly in the middle of combat, but a Shield can be equipped or removed with a [[Utilise|Utilise action]].
