Unlike Experience Points and treasure, Heroic Inspiration doesn’t stay with a character indefinitely. It is earned and used on a per-session basis. Brave last stands, protecting innocents, clever strategies, or using a well-timed spell to save the day can all earn a character Heroic Inspiration.

If you have Heroic Inspiration, you can expend 1 Heroic Inspiration to reroll any die immediately after the outcome is known. This can include a die rolled by another player character or by an NPC, including an enemy. The new roll must be used.

Heroic Inspiration can be used only once on any one die roll.

You can have a maximum of 3 Heroic Inspiration at a time. At the start of each new session, if you have more than 1 Heroic Inspiration, your total resets to 1, and any excess Heroic Inspiration is lost.
