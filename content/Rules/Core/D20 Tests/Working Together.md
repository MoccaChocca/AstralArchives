Sometimes two or more characters team up to attempt a task. The character who's leading the effort, or the one with the highest ability modifier, can make an [[Ability Checks|ability check]] with [[Advantage and Disadvantage|advantage]]. This reflects the help provided by the other characters. In combat, this requires the [[Help|Help action]].

A character can only provide help if the task is one that they are capable in. If the task involves a skill, tool, or language, a character must be proficient with that skill, tool, or language to provide help.

For the duration of the task, both characters must be able to act and communicate effectively. A character can provide help only when two or more creatures working together would reasonably make the task easier. Some tasks, such as threading a needle, are no easier with help.
