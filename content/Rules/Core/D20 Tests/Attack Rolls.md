An attack roll is a type of [[D20 Tests|D20 Test]] used to determine whether an attack hits a target.

When a creature makes an attack, it rolls a d20 and adds the appropriate modifiers. If the total equals or exceeds the target’s Armor Class, the attack hits. Otherwise, the attack misses.

### Making an Attack Roll  
To make an attack roll, roll a d20 and add the relevant ability modifier. If the creature is proficient with the weapon or spell used to make the attack, it also adds its [[proficiency bonus]].  

The ability modifier used for the attack roll depends on the type of attack.  

#### Melee Weapon Attacks  
A melee weapon attack usually uses [[Strength]] for the attack roll and damage roll.  

#### Ranged Weapon Attacks  
A ranged weapon attack usually uses [[Dexterity]] for the attack roll and damage roll.  
  
A thrown weapon uses the same ability modifier for the attack roll and damage roll that would be used for a melee attack with that weapon. For example, a thrown handaxe uses [[Strength]], while a thrown dagger can use [[Strength]] or [[Dexterity]] because it has the Finesse property.
  
#### Spell Attacks  
Some spells require the caster to make a spell attack. A spell attack uses the caster’s spell attack modifier.  

A spell tells the caster whether it uses a melee spell attack or a ranged spell attack.  

### Rolling a 1 or 20  
If the d20 roll for an attack is a 1, the attack misses, regardless of any modifiers or the target’s Armor Class.  
  
If the d20 roll for an attack is a 20, the attack hits, regardless of any modifiers or the target’s Armor Class. This is called a [[Critical Hits|critical hit]].
