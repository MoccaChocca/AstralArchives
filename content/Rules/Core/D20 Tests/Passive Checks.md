A passive check is a special kind of ability check that doesn't involve any die rolls. Such a check can represent the average result for a task done repeatedly, such as searching for secret doors over and over again, or can be used when the DM wants to secretly determine whether the characters succeed at something without rolling dice, such as noticing a [[hidden]] monster.

Here's how to determine a character's total for a passive check:

**10 + all modifiers that normally apply to the check**

If the character has [[Advantage and Disadvantage|advantage]] on the check, add 5. For [[Advantage and Disadvantage|disadvantage]], subtract 5. Your passive check total is referred to as a **score**.

For example, if a 1st-level character has a Wisdom of 15 and proficiency in Perception, they have a passive [[Wisdom|Wisdom (Perception)]] score of 14.
