The divergent nature of magic wielders and their magical practices mean there's no one way to cast a spell. Whether out of competitiveness or a desire to personalise their spellwork, many magic users have added or substituted material components beyond the conventional.

Spell Catalysts are consumable magic items used to alter specific spells. Each catalyst is linked to one or more spells, as described in the catalyst’s entry.

When you take the [[Magic action]] to [[Magic Action|cast a spell]] named in a catalyst’s description, you can expend that catalyst as an additional material component for the spell, causing the spell to gain the catalyst’s effect. If the spell doesn’t normally have a material component, the catalyst becomes the spell’s material component for that casting. A catalyst is consumed when used, and it can’t be replaced by a spellcasting focus or component pouch.

You must be able to provide the spell’s [[components]] to use a catalyst with it. If you [[Magic Action|cast a spell]] without providing [[components]], you can’t use a catalyst with that casting.

Unless a catalyst says otherwise, you can use only one catalyst with a spell when you cast it.
