When you score a critical hit, you get to roll extra dice for the attack's damage against the target. Roll all of the attack's damage normally, then double it to determine the damage of your critical hit.

For example, if you score a critical hit with a dagger, roll 1d4 for the damage then add your relevant ability modifier, then double your total.. If the attack involves other damage dice, such as from the rogue's Sneak Attack feature, you double this damage as well.
