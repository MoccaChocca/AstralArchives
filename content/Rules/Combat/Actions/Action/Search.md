When you take the Search action, you make a Wisdom check to discern something that isn’t obvious. The Search table suggests which skills are applicable when you take this action, depending on what you’re trying to detect.

#### Search
| Skill      | Thing to Detect                      |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Insight    | Creature’s state of mind             |
| Medicine   | Creature’s ailment or cause of death |
| Perception | Concealed creature or object         |
| Survival   | Tracks or food                       |
### Search - [[Bonus Action]]
Unless a rule (or the DM) says otherwise, you can take the Search action as a Bonus Action. When you do so, your search is brief and focused, limiting what you can notice or find.  
  
For example, a Search action taken as an action might reveal that a room contains a hidden door, a blood trail leading beneath a rug, and fresh bootprints near the window. If you take the Search action as a Bonus Action, you might notice only the blood trail you are specifically looking for, or only the hidden door if that is where you focus your attention.
