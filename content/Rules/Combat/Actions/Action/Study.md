When you take the Study action, you make an [[Intelligence]] check to study your memory, a book, a clue, or another source of knowledge and call to mind an important piece of information about it.

The Areas of Knowledge table suggests which skills are applicable to various areas of knowledge.

### Study - [[Bonus Action]]
Unless a rule (or the DM) says otherwise, you can take the Study action as a Bonus Action. When you do so, your examination is brief and focused, limiting what you can learn.

For example, a Study action taken as an action might reveal that five bandits are affected by the *Seeming* spell. If you take the Study action as a Bonus Action, you might learn only that one bandit you focus on is affected by the spell.
