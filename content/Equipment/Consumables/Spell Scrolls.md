A Spell Scroll bears the words of a single spell, written in a mystical cipher. To use a Spell Scroll, you must have the Spellcasting or Pact Magic feature.

While holding the scroll, you can read it and cast the spell written on it without [[Components|Material components]]. Casting the spell requires the spell’s normal [[casting time]]. Once the spell is cast, the scroll crumbles to dust. If the casting is interrupted, the scroll isn’t lost.

If the spell is on one of your spell lists and is of a level you can normally cast, you cast the spell without making an [[Ability Checks|ability check]].

If the spell isn’t on any of your spell lists, or if the spell is on one of your spell lists but is of a higher level than you can normally cast, you must make an [[Ability Checks|ability check]] using your spellcasting ability. If you have more than one spellcasting ability, choose which one to use for this check. The DC equals 10 plus the spell’s level. On a failed check, the spell disappears from the scroll with no other effect. If the spell is a cantrip, its level is 0.
