*Spell Catalyst, uncommon*

This fine black powder clings to metal and faintly sparks when exposed to charged air. Mages scatter it through the path of a spell to make lightning hunt for armour, weapons, and other conductive surfaces.

***Catalyst Spell:*** _[[Lightning Bolt]]_ 

When you cast _[[lightning bolt]]_, you can expend this catalyst as an additional material component for the spell. 

A creature wearing metal armour has [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on its [[Saving Throws|saving throw]] against the spell.
