*Spell Catalyst, rare*

This heavy lump of magnetised iron is veined with silver-blue streaks. When used in spellcasting, it pulls lightning out of its natural path and drags the strike toward armoured creatures.

***Catalyst Spells:*** _[[Call Lightning]]_, _[[Lightning Bolt]]_

When you cast _[[call lightning]]_ or _[[lightning bolt]]_, you can expend this catalyst as an additional material component for the spell. 

A creature wearing metal armour has [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on its [[Saving Throws|saving throw]] against the spell.

If you use this catalyst with _[[call lightning]]_, the effect applies only to the initial bolt of lightning called as part of casting the spell. On each of your later turns while the spell lasts, when you take the [[Magic action]] to call lightning again, you can expend another Ferrous Chunk to cause creatures wearing metal armour to have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on their [[saving throws]] against that bolt.
