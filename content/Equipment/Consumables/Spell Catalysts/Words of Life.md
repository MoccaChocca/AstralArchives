*Spell Catalyst, uncommon*

These tiny parchment slips are inscribed with restorative invocations, each one written in ink mixed with powdered pearl and dried medicinal herbs.

***Catalyst Spell:*** _[[Mass Healing Word]]_

When you cast _[[mass healing word]]_, you can expend this catalyst as an additional material component for the spell.

Roll the spell’s healing once for each creature targeted by the spell, then combine the results into a healing pool. You divide that healing among the spell’s targets, restoring at least 1 [[Hit Points|Hit Point]] to each target.
