*Spell Catalyst, uncommon*

This thin copper rod hums faintly when held near charged air, and its surface is etched with branching channels that guide unstable magical current.

***Catalyst Spell:*** _[[Lightning Bolt]]_

When you cast _[[lightning bolt]]_, you can expend this catalyst as an additional material component for the spell.

If the spell’s [[Areas of Effect|line]] strikes a solid surface made of stone, metal, or similarly hard material before reaching the end of the line, the line ricochets from that surface. The new direction of the line is directly perpendicular from the point of impact.

The spell’s total length doesn’t increase. A creature can be affected by the spell only once during this casting.
