*Potion, Common*

***Weight.*** —
***Value.*** 45 GP

When you drink this potion, you gain a [[Rules/Core/Movement/Movement|Climb Speed]] equal to your Speed for 1 hour. During this time, you have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on [[Strength|Strength (Athletics)]] checks to climb.

This potion is separated into brown, silver, and grey layers resembling bands of stone. Shaking the bottle fails to mix the colours.
