*Potion, Uncommon*

***Weight.*** —
***Value.*** 325 GP

This concoction looks, smells, and tastes like a [[Potion of Healing]] or another beneficial potion. However, it is actually poison masked by illusion magic. [[Identify]] reveals its true nature.

If you drink this potion, you take 4d6 Poison damage and must succeed on a DC 13 [[Saving Throws|Constitution saving throw]] or have the [[Poisoned]] condition for 1 hour.
