*Weapon ([[Longbow]] or [[Shortbow]]), Very Rare (requires attunement)*

***Weight.*** 2 lb.
***Value.*** 5,500 GP

When you nock an arrow on this bow, it whispers in Elvish, “Swift defeat to my enemies.” When you use this weapon to make a ranged attack, you can utter or sign the following command words: “Swift death to you who have wronged me.” The target of your attack becomes your sworn enemy until it dies or until dawn 7 days later. You can have only one such sworn enemy at a time. When your sworn enemy dies, you can choose a new one after the next dawn.

When you make a ranged [[Attack Rolls|attack roll]] with this weapon against your sworn enemy, you have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on the roll. In addition, your target gains no benefit from [[Cover|Half Cover]] or [[Cover|Three-Quarters Cover]], and you suffer no [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] due to long range. If the attack hits, your sworn enemy takes an extra 3d6 Piercing damage.

While your sworn enemy lives, you have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on [[attack rolls]] with all other weapons.
