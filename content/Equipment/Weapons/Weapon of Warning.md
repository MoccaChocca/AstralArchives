*Weapon (Any Simple or Martial), Uncommon (requires attunement)*

***Weight.*** —
***Value.*** 1,100 GP

As long as this weapon is within your reach and you are attuned to it, you and allies within 30 feet of you gain the following benefits.

**_Alarm._** The weapon magically awakens each subject who is sleeping naturally when combat begins. This benefit doesn’t wake a subject from magically induced sleep.

**_Supernatural Readiness._** Each subject has [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on its [[Initiative]] rolls.
