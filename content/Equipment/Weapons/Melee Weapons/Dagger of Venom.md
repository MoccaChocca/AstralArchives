*Weapon ([[Dagger]]), Rare*

***Weight.*** 1 lb.
***Value.*** 1,450 GP

***Base Weapon.*** [[Dagger]]

You gain a +1 bonus to [[attack rolls]] and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

You can take a [[Bonus Action]] to magically coat the blade with poison. The poison remains for 1 minute or until an attack using this weapon hits a creature. That creature must succeed on a DC 15 [[Saving Throws|Constitution saving throw]] or take 2d10 Poison damage and have the [[Poisoned]] condition for 1 minute. The weapon can’t be used this way again until the next dawn.
