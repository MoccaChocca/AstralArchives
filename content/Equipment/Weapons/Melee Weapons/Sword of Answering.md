*Weapon ([[Longsword]]), Legendary (requires attunement)*

***Weight.*** 3 lb.
***Value.*** 14,000 GP

You gain a +3 bonus to [[Attack Rolls|attack rolls]] and damage rolls made with this sword. In addition, while you hold the sword, you can take a [[Reaction]] to make one melee attack with it against any creature in your reach that deals damage to you. You have Advantage on the [[Attack Rolls|attack roll]], and any damage dealt with this special attack ignores any [[Immunity]] or [[Damage Resistance and Vulnerability|Resistance]] the target has to that damage.
