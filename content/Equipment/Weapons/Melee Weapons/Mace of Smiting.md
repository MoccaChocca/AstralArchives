*Weapon ([[Mace]]), Rare*

***Weight.*** 4 lb.
***Value.*** 1,500 GP

You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. The bonus increases to +3 when you use the weapon to attack a Construct.

When you roll a 20 on an [[Attack Rolls|attack roll]] made with this weapon, the target takes an extra 7 Bludgeoning damage, or 14 Bludgeoning damage if it’s a Construct. If a Construct has 25 [[Hit Points]] or fewer after taking this damage, it is destroyed.
