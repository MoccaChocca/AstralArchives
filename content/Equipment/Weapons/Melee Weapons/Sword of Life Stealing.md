*Weapon ([[Glaive]], [[Greatsword]], [[Longsword]], [[Rapier]], [[Scimitar]], or [[Shortsword]]), Rare (requires attunement)*

***Weight.*** 6 lb.
***Value.*** 1,700 GP

When you attack a creature with this magic weapon and roll a 20 on the d20 for the [[Attack Rolls|attack roll]], that target takes an extra 15 Necrotic damage if it isn’t a Construct or an Undead, and you gain [[Temporary Hit Points]] equal to the amount of Necrotic damage taken.
