*Weapon (Any Melee Weapon), Rare (requires attunement)*

***Weight.*** —
***Value.*** 2,200 GP

While holding this magic weapon, you can take a Bonus Action and use a command word to cause flames to engulf the damage-dealing part of the weapon. These flames shed [[Spells/0 - Cantrips/Light|Bright Light]] in a 40-foot radius and [[Spells/0 - Cantrips/Light|Dim Light]] for an additional 40 feet. While the weapon is ablaze, it deals an extra 2d6 Fire damage on a hit. The flames last until you take a Bonus Action to issue the command again or until you drop, stow, or sheathe the weapon.
