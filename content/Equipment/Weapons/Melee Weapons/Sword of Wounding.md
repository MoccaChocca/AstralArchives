*Weapon ([[Glaive]], [[Greatsword]], [[Longsword]], [[Rapier]], [[Scimitar]], or [[Shortsword]]), Rare (requires attunement)*

***Weight.*** 6 lb.
***Value.*** 1,700 GP

When you hit a creature with an attack using this magic weapon, the target takes an extra 2d6 Necrotic damage and must succeed on a DC 15 [[Saving Throws|Constitution saving throw]] or be unable to regain [[Hit Points]] for 1 hour. The target repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
