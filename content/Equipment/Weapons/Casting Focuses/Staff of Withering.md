*Staff, Rare (requires attunement)*

***Weight.*** 4 lb.
***Value.*** 1,700 GP

This staff has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn.

The staff can be wielded as a magic [[Quarterstaff]]. On a hit, it deals damage as a normal [[Quarterstaff]], and you can expend 1 charge to deal an extra 2d10 Necrotic damage to the target and force it to make a DC 15 [[Saving Throws|Constitution saving throw]]. On a failed save, the target has [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] for 1 hour on any [[Ability Checks|ability check]] or [[Saving Throws|saving throw]] that uses [[Strength]] or [[Constitution]].
