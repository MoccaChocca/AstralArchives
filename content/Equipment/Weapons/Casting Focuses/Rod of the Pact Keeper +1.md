*Rod, Uncommon (requires attunement by a Warlock)*

***Weight.*** —
***Value.*** 2,200 GP

While holding this rod, you gain a +1 bonus to spell [[Attack Rolls|attack rolls]] and to the [[Saving Throws|saving throw]] DCs of your Warlock spells.

In addition, you can regain one spell slot as a [[Magic action]] while holding the rod. You can’t use this property again until you finish a [[Resting|Long Rest]].
