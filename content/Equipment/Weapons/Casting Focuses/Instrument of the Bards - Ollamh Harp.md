*Wondrous Item, Legendary (requires attunement by a Bard)*

***Weight.*** —
***Value.*** 12,000 GP

This _Instrument of the Bards_ is a Ollamh harp. It is superior to an ordinary instrument in every way. A creature that attempts to play the instrument without being attuned to it must succeed on a DC 15 [[Saving Throws|Wisdom saving throw]] or take 2d4 Psychic damage.

You can play the instrument to cast one of its spells. Once the instrument has been used to cast a spell, it can’t be used to cast that spell again until the next dawn. The spells use your spellcasting ability and spell save DC.

**_Spells._** *[[Fly]]*, *[[Invisibility]]*, *[[Levitate]]*, *[[Protection from Evil and Good]]*, *[[Confusion]]*, *[[Control Weather]]*, and *[[Fire Storm]]*.
