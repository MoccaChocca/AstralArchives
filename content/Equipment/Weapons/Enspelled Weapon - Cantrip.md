*Weapon (Any Simple or Martial), Uncommon (requires attunement)*

***Weight.*** —
***Value.*** 750 GP

Bound into this weapon is a cantrip. The spell is determined when the weapon is created and must belong to the Conjuration, Divination, Evocation, Necromancy, or Transmutation school of magic. The weapon has 6 charges and regains 1d6 expended charges daily at dawn. While holding the weapon, you can expend 1 charge to cast its spell.

For this item, the spell save DC is 13, and the spell attack bonus is +5.
