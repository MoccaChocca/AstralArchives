*Weapon (Any Simple or Martial), Legendary (requires attunement)*

***Weight.*** —
***Value.*** 13,000 GP

You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this [[magic weapon]]. When you hit a Fiend or an Undead with it, that creature takes an extra 2d10 Radiant damage.

While you hold the drawn weapon, it creates a 10-foot [[Areas of Effect|Emanation]] originating from you. You and all creatures Friendly to you in the Emanation have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on [[saving throws]] against spells and other magical effects. If you have 17 or more levels in the [[Paladin]] class, the size of the [[Areas of Effect|Emanation]] increases to 30 feet.
