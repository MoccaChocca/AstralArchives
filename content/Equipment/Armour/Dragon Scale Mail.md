*Armour ([[Scale Mail]]), Very Rare (requires attunement)*

***Weight.*** 45 lb.
***Value.*** 5,500 GP

_Dragon Scale Mail_ is made of the scales of one kind of dragon. Sometimes dragons collect their cast-off scales and gift them. Other times, hunters carefully preserve the hide of a dead dragon. In either case, _Dragon Scale Mail_ is highly valued.

While wearing this armour, you gain a +1 bonus to Armour Class, you have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on [[saving throws]] against the breath weapons of Dragons, and you have [[Damage Resistance and Vulnerability|Resistance]] to one damage type determined by the kind of dragon that provided the scales (see the accompanying table).

Additionally, you can focus your senses as a [[Magic action]] to discern the distance and direction to the closest dragon within 30 miles of yourself that is of the same type as the armour. This action can’t be used again until the next dawn.

|Dragon|Resistance|
|---|---|
|Black|Acid|
|Blue|Lightning|
|Brass|Fire|
|Bronze|Lightning|
|Copper|Acid|
|Gold|Fire|
|Green|Poison|
|Red|Fire|
|Silver|Cold|
|White|Cold|
