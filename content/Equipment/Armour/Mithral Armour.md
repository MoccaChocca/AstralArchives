*Armour (Any Medium or Heavy, Except Hide Armour), Uncommon*

***Weight.*** —
***Value.*** 575 GP

Mithral is a light, flexible metal. Armour made of this substance can be worn under normal clothes. If the armour normally imposes [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on [[Dexterity|Dexterity (Stealth)]] checks or has a [[Strength]] requirement, the mithral version of the armour doesn’t.
