*Armour ([[Shield]]), Uncommon*

***Weight.*** 6 lb.
***Value.*** 1,200 GP

You gain a +1 bonus to [[Armour Class]] while wielding this shield.

The shield has 4 charges. While holding it, when a Large or smaller creature within 5 feet of you hits you with a melee [[Attack Rolls|attack roll]], you can take a [[Reaction]] to expend 1 of the shield’s charges and push the attacker up to 15 feet straight away. The shield regains 1d4 expended charges daily at dawn.
