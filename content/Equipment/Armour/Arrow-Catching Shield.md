*Armour ([[Equipment/Mundane Equipment/Armour/Shield|Shield]]), Rare (requires attunement)*

***Weight.*** 6 pounds
***Value.*** 1,700 GP

You gain a +2 bonus to Armour Class against ranged [[attack rolls]] while you wield this Shield. This bonus is in addition to the Shield’s normal bonus to AC.

Whenever an attacker makes a ranged [[Attack Rolls|attack roll]] against a target within 5 feet of you, you can take a [[Reaction]] to become the target of the attack instead.
