*Armour (Any Light, Medium, or Heavy), Very Rare (requires attunement)*

***Weight.*** —
***Value.*** 4,700 GP

While wearing this armour, you gain a +1 bonus to Armour Class, and you know Abyssal. In addition, the armour’s clawed gauntlets allow your Unarmed Strikes to deal 1d8 Slashing damage instead of the usual Bludgeoning damage, and you gain a +1 bonus to the attack and damage rolls of your Unarmed Strikes.

**_Curse._** Once you don this cursed armour, you can’t doff it unless you are targeted by a [[Remove Curse]] spell or similar magic. While wearing the armour, you have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on attack rolls against demons and on saving throws against their spells and special abilities.
