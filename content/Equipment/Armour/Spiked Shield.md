*Armour (shield), Uncommon (requires attunement)*

***Weight.*** 6 lb.
***Value.*** 950 GP

Crude metal spikes adorn this magic Shield. It has the following properties that you can use while holding it.

**_Shield Bash._** When you take the Attack action, you can make one of the [[Attack Rolls|attack rolls]] using the Shield against a target within 5 feet of yourself. Apply your Proficiency Bonus and Strength modifier to the [[Attack Rolls|attack roll]]. On a hit, the Shield deals Piercing damage equal to 2d6 plus your Strength modifier.

**_Spike Salvo._** As a [[Magic action]], you can cause spikes to erupt from the Shield in a 30-foot Cone. Each creature in the Cone makes a DC 13 [[Saving Throws|Dexterity saving throw]], taking 3d6 Piercing damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. Once this property is used, it can’t be used again until the next dawn.
