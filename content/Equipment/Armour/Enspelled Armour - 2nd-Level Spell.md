*Armour (Any Light, Medium, or Heavy), Rare (requires attunement)*

***Weight.*** —
***Value.*** 1,700 GP

Bound into this armour is a level 2 spell. The spell is determined when the armour is created and must belong to the Abjuration or Illusion school of magic. The armour has 6 charges and regains 1d6 expended charges daily at dawn. While wearing the armour, you can expend 1 charge to cast its spell.

For this item, the spell save DC is 13, and the spell attack bonus is +5.
