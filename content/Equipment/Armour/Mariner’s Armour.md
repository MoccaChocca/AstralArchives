*Armour (Any Light, Medium, or Heavy), Uncommon*

***Weight.*** —
***Value.*** 650 GP

While wearing this armour, you have a [[Rules/Core/Movement/Movement|Swim Speed]] equal to your Speed. In addition, if you start your turn underwater with 0 [[Hit Points]], you immediately regain 1d4 [[Hit Points]]. The armour can’t heal anyone again until the next dawn.

The armour is decorated with fish and shell motifs.
