*Wondrous Item, Uncommon (requires attunement)*

***Weight.*** —
***Value.*** 1,200 GP

While wearing this helm, you have [[Setting/Languages/Secret Languages/Telepathy|telepathy]] with a range of 30 feet, and you can cast [[Detect Thoughts]] or [[Suggestion]] (save DC 13) from the helm. Once either spell is cast from the helm, that spell can’t be cast from it again until the next dawn.
