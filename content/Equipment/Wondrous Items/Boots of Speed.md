*Wondrous Item, Rare (requires attunement)*

***Weight.*** —
***Value.*** 1,700 GP

While you wear these boots, you can take a [[Bonus Action]] to click the boots’ heels together. If you do, the boots double your Speed, and any creature that makes an [[Opportunity Attacks|Opportunity Attack]] against you has [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on the attack roll. If you click your heels together again, you end the effect.

When you’ve used the boots’ property for a total of 10 minutes, the magic ceases to function for you until you finish a Long Rest.
