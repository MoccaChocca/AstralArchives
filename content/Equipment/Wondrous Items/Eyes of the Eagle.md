*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*

***Weight.*** —
***Value.*** 500 GP

These crystal lenses fit over the eyes. While wearing them, you have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on [[Wisdom|Wisdom (Perception)]] checks that rely on sight. In conditions of clear visibility, you can make out details of even extremely distant creatures and objects as small as 2 feet across.
