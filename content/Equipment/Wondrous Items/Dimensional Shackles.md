*Wondrous Item, Rare*

***Weight.*** —
***Value.*** 2,150 GP

You can take a [[Utilise|Utilise action]] to place these shackles on a creature that has the [[Incapacitated]] condition. The shackles adjust to fit a creature of Small to Large size. The shackles prevent a creature bound by them from using any method of extradimensional movement, including teleportation or travel to a different plane of existence. They don’t prevent the creature from passing through an interdimensional portal.

You and any creature you designate when you use the shackles can take a [[Utilise|Utilise action]] to remove them. Once every 30 days, the bound creature can make a DC 30 [[Strength|Strength (Athletics)]] check. On a successful check, the creature breaks free and destroys the shackles.
