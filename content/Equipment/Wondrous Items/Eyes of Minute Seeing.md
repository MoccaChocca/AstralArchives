*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*

***Weight.*** —
***Value.*** 500 GP

These crystal lenses fit over the eyes. While wearing them, your vision improves significantly out to a range of 1 foot, granting you [[Darkvision]] within that range and [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on [[Intelligence|Intelligence (Investigation)]] checks made to examine something within that range.
