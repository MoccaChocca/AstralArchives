*Wondrous Item, Uncommon (requires attunement by a Humanoid)*

***Weight.*** —
***Value.*** 500 GP

A dire wolf’s tooth dangles from this simple cord necklace. You gain a +1 bonus to ability checks and [[Saving Throws|saving throws]] while you wear this necklace.

**_Curse._** This necklace is cursed. Attuning to the necklace curses you; this curse can’t be removed until Harkon Lukas dies. As long as you remain cursed, you become a Werewolf serving Harkon Lukas under the DM’s control during the night of a full moon.
