*Wondrous Item, Rare (requires attunement)*

***Weight.*** —
***Value.*** 1,800 GP

The central jewel of this butterfly-shaped brooch swirls with multicolored iridescence.

The brooch has 3 charges and regains all expended charges daily at dawn. As a [[Reaction]] when a creature you can see within 60 feet of you hits a target with an [[Attack Rolls|attack roll]] and deals damage, you can expend 1 charge and change one damage type the attack deals to one of the following damage types: Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Poison, or Thunder.
