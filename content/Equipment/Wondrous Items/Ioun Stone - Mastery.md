*Wondrous Item, Legendary (requires attunement)*

***Weight.*** —
***Value.*** 16,000 GP

***Other Variants.*** [[Ioun Stone - Absorption|Absorption]], [[Ioun Stone - Agility|Agility]], [[Ioun Stone - Awareness|Awareness]], [[Ioun Stone - Fortitude|Fortitude]], [[Ioun Stone - Greater Absorption|Greater Absorption]], [[Ioun Stone - Insight|Insight]], [[Ioun Stone - Intellect|Intellect]], [[Ioun Stone - Leadership|Leadership]], [[Ioun Stone - Protection|Protection]], [[Ioun Stone - Regeneration|Regeneration]], [[Ioun Stone - Reserve|Reserve]], [[Ioun Stone - Strength|Strength]], [[Ioun Stone - Sustenance|Sustenance]].


Roughly marble sized, _Ioun Stones_ are named after Ioun, a god of knowledge and prophecy revered on some worlds. Many types of _Ioun Stones_ exist, each type a distinct combination of shape and colour.

When you take a [[Magic action]] to toss an _Ioun Stone_ into the air, the stone orbits your head at a distance of 1d3 feet, conferring its benefit to you while doing so. You can have up to three _Ioun Stones_ orbiting your head at the same time.

Each _Ioun Stone_ orbiting your head is considered to be an object you are wearing. The orbiting stone avoids contact with other creatures and objects, adjusting its orbit to avoid collisions and thwarting all attempts by other creatures to attack or snatch it.

As a [[Utilise|Utilise action]], you can seize and stow any number of _Ioun Stones_ orbiting your head. If your Attunement to an _Ioun Stone_ ends while it’s orbiting your head, the stone falls as though you had dropped it.

The type of stone determines its rarity and effects.

#### Mastery (Legendary)

Your [[Proficiency Bonus]] increases by 1 while this pale green prism orbits your head.
