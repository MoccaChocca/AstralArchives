*Wondrous Item, Common*

***Weight.*** —
***Value.*** 100 GP

This 50-foot coil of rope can repair itself when cut into any number of smaller pieces. As a [[Magic action]], you can cause all pieces of the rope that are in contact with each other and not otherwise in use to knit back together. A _Rope of Mending_ is forever shortened if a section of it is lost or destroyed.
