*Wondrous Item, Rare*

***Weight.*** —
***Value.*** 2,150 GP

This cape smells faintly of brimstone. While wearing it, you can use it to cast *[[Dimension Door]]* as a [[Magic action]]. This property can’t be used again until the next dawn.

When you teleport with that spell, you leave behind a cloud of smoke. The space you left is [[Vision|Lightly Obscured]] by that smoke until the end of your next turn.
