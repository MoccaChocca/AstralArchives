*Ring, Uncommon (requires attunement)*

***Weight.*** —
***Value.*** 950 GP

The item has 4 charges. When you fail a [[Saving Throws|Constitution saving throw]] to maintain Concentration, you can take a [[Reaction]] and expend 1 of the item’s charges to succeed instead. The item regains 1d4 expended charges daily at dawn.
