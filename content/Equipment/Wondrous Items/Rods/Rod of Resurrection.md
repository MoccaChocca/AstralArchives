*Rod, Legendary (requires attunement)*

***Weight.*** 2 lb.
***Value.*** 13,000 GP

The rod has 5 charges. While you hold it, you can use a [[Magic action]] to cast one of the following spells from it: *[[Heal]]* (expends 1 charge) or *[[Resurrection]]* (expends 5 charges).

The rod regains 1 expended charge daily at dawn. If you expend the last charge, roll 1d20. On a 1, the rod disappears in a harmless burst of radiance.
