*Rod, Very Rare (requires attunement)*

***Weight.*** 2 lb.
***Value.*** 6,000 GP

This rod has the following properties.

**_Alertness._** While holding the rod, you have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on [[Wisdom|Wisdom (Perception)]] checks and on [[Initiative]] rolls.

**_Spells._** While holding the rod, you can cast the following spells from it:

- *[[Detect Evil and Good]]*
- *[[Detect Magic]]*
- *[[Detect Poison and Disease]]*
- *[[See Invisibility]]*

**_Protective Aura._** As a [[Magic action]], you can plant the haft end of the rod in the ground, whereupon the rod’s head sheds [[Spells/0 - Cantrips/Light|Bright Light]] in a 60-foot radius and [[Spells/0 - Cantrips/Light|Dim Light]] for an additional 60 feet. While in that Bright Light, you and your allies gain a +1 bonus to Armour Class and [[Saving Throws|saving throws]] and can sense the location of any [[Invisible]] creature that is also in the Bright Light.

The rod’s head stops glowing and the effect ends after 10 minutes or when a creature takes a [[Magic action]] to pull the rod from the ground. Once used, this property can’t be used again until the next dawn.
