*Ring, Rare (requires attunement)*

***Weight.*** —
***Value.*** 1,700 GP

This ring has 3 charges, and it regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. When you fail a [[Saving Throws|Dexterity saving throw]] while wearing the ring, you can take a [[Reaction]] to expend 1 charge to succeed on that save instead.
