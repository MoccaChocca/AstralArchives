*Ring, Rare (requires attunement)*

***Weight.*** —
***Value.*** 1,700 GP

While wearing this ring, you can take a [[Magic Action|Magic action]] to gain X-ray vision with a range of 30 feet for 1 minute. To you, solid objects within that radius appear transparent and don’t prevent light from passing through them. The vision can penetrate 1 foot of stone, 1 inch of common metal, or up to 3 feet of wood or dirt. Thicker substances or a thin sheet of lead block the vision.

Whenever you use the ring again before taking a [[Resting|Long Rest]], you must succeed on a DC 15 [[Saving Throws|Constitution saving throw]] or gain 1 level of [[Exhaustion]].
