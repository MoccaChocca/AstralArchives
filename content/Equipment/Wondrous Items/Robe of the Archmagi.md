*Wondrous Item, Legendary (requires attunement)*

***Weight.*** —
***Value.*** 16,000 GP

This elegant garment is made from exquisite cloth and adorned with runes.

You gain these benefits while wearing the robe.

**_Armour._** If you aren’t wearing armour, your base Armour Class is 15 plus your [[Dexterity]] modifier.

**_Magic [[Damage Resistance and Vulnerability|Resistance]]._** You have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**_War Mage._** Your spell save DC and spell attack bonus each increase by 2.
