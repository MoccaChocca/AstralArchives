*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*

***Weight.*** —
***Value.*** 625 GP

While lit, this hooded lantern burns for 6 hours on 1 pint of oil, shedding [[Spells/0 - Cantrips/Light|Bright Light]] in a 30-foot radius and [[Spells/0 - Cantrips/Light|Dim Light]] for an additional 30 feet. [[Invisible]] creatures and objects are visible as long as they are in the lantern’s Bright Light. You can take a [[Utilise|Utilise action]] to lower the hood, reducing the lantern’s light to Dim Light in a 5-foot radius.
