*Wondrous Item, Legendary*

***Weight.*** —
***Value.*** 15,000 GP

This tube holds milky liquid with a strong alcohol smell. When found, a tube contains 1d6 + 1 ounces.

You can take a [[Utilise|Utilise action]] to pour 1 or more ounces of solvent from the tube onto a surface within reach. Each ounce instantly dissolves up to 1 square foot of adhesive it touches, including [[Sovereign Glue]].
