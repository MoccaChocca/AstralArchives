*Wondrous Item, Common (requires attunement by a Wizard)*

***Weight.*** —
***Value.*** 150 GP

This cone-shaped hat is adorned with moons and stars. While you are wearing it, you gain the following benefits.

**_Spellcasting Focus._** You can use the hat as a Spellcasting Focus for your Wizard spells.

**_Unknown Spell._** As a [[Magic action]], you can try to cast a cantrip that you don’t know. The cantrip must be on the Wizard spell list and have a casting time of an action, and you make a DC 10 [[Intelligence|Intelligence (Arcana)]] check. On a successful check, you cast the spell. On a failed check, the spell fails, and the action used to cast the spell is wasted. In either case, you can’t use this property again until you finish a [[Resting|Long Rest]].
