*Ring, Common*

***Weight.*** —
***Value.*** 100 GP

"Lamps are a liability on adventures. Delicate and prone to oil spillage, lamps fill a hand that should otherwise be wielding a weapon or casting a spell. Once you try one of our elegant and practical rings, you’ll never go back to cumbersome flint and fire."

While the cover on this ring is open, the ring produces a flame that creates no heat and consumes no fuel. It sheds [[Light|Bright Light]] in a 20-foot radius and [[Light|Dim Light]] for an additional 20 feet. As a [[Bonus Action]], you can close the cover, smothering the flame, or open it again.
