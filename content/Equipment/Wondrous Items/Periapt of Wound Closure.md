*Wondrous Item, Uncommon (requires attunement)*

***Weight.*** —
***Value.*** 500 GP

While wearing this pendant, you gain the following benefits.

**_Life Preservation._** Whenever you make a Death Saving Throw, you can change a roll of 9 or lower to a 10, turning a failed save into a successful one.

**_Natural Healing Boost._** Whenever you roll a [[Hit Points|Hit Point Die]] to regain [[Hit Points]], double the number of [[Hit Points]] it restores.
