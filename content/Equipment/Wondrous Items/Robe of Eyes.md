*Wondrous Item, Rare (requires attunement)*

***Weight.*** —
***Value.*** 1,700 GP

This robe is adorned with eyelike patterns. While you wear the robe, you gain the following benefits:

**All-Around Vision.** The robe gives you [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on [[Wisdom|Wisdom (Perception)]] checks that rely on sight.

**Special Senses.** You have [[Darkvision]] and Truesight, both with a range of 120 feet.

**_Drawbacks._** A [[Spells/0 - Cantrips/Light]] spell cast on the robe or a *[[Daylight]]* spell cast within 5 feet of the robe gives you the [[Blinded]] condition for 1 minute. At the end of each of your turns, you make a [[Saving Throws|Constitution saving throw]] (DC 11 for _Light_ or DC 15 for _Daylight_), ending the condition on yourself on a success.
