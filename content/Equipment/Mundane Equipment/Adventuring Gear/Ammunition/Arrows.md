*Ammunition, Consumable, Mundane*

Arrows are fletched shafts designed to be fired from bows.

***Value.*** 1 GP
***Weight.*** 1 lb.
***Amount.*** 20
***Storage.*** [[Quiver]]

Ammunition is required by a weapon that has the [[Rules/Equipment/Weapon Properties/Ammunition|Ammunition]] property. A weapon’s description specifies the type of ammunition used by the weapon.
