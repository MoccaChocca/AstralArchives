*Adventuring Gear, Consumable, Mundane*

A typical Candle is a small wax taper with a wick used for weak, steady light.

***Value.*** 1 CP
***Weight.*** -

For 1 hour, a lit Candle sheds [[Vision|Bright Light]] in a 5-foot radius and [[Vision|Dim Light]] for an additional 5 feet.
