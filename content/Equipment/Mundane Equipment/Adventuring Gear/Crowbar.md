*Adventuring Gear, Mundane*

***Value.*** 2 GP
***Weight.*** 5 pounds

A Crowbar is a sturdy iron lever used for prying open doors, crates, lids, and other secured objects.

Using a Crowbar gives you [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on [[Strength]] checks where the Crowbar’s leverage can be applied.
