*Adventuring Gear, Mundane*

A Map is a drawn representation of a region, settlement, dungeon, or route.

***Value.*** 1 GP
***Weight.*** -

If you consult an accurate Map, you gain a +5 bonus to [[Wisdom|Wisdom (Survival)]] checks you make to find your way in the place represented on it.
