*Adventuring Gear, Mundane*

A Magnifying Glass is a polished lens mounted in a frame for inspecting fine details.

***Value.*** 100 GP
***Weight.*** -

A Magnifying Glass grants [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on any ability check made to appraise or inspect a highly detailed item. Lighting a fire with a Magnifying Glass requires light as bright as sunlight to focus, tinder to ignite, and about 5 minutes for the fire to ignite.
