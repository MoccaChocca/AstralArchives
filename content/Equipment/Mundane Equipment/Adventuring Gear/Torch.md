*Adventuring Gear, Consumable, Mundane*

A Torch is a wooden brand wrapped with pitch-soaked cloth for portable flame and light.

***Value.*** 1 CP
***Weight.*** 1 lb.

A Torch burns for 1 hour, casting [[Vision|Bright Light]] in a 20-foot radius and [[Vision|Dim Light]] for an additional 20 feet. When you take the [[Attack action]], you can attack with the Torch, using it as a Simple Melee weapon. On a hit, the target takes 1 Fire damage.
