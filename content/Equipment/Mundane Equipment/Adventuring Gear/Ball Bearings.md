*Adventuring Gear, Mundane*

***Value.*** 1 GP
***Weight.*** 2 pounds

A pouch of Ball Bearings contains small metal spheres that can be scattered across the ground to trip unwary creatures.

As a [[Utilise|Utilise action]], you can spill Ball Bearings from their pouch. They spread to cover a level, 10-foot-square area within 10 feet of yourself. A creature that enters this area for the first time on a turn must succeed on a DC 10 [[Saving Throws|Dexterity saving throw]] or have the [[Prone]] condition. It takes 10 minutes to recover the Ball Bearings.
