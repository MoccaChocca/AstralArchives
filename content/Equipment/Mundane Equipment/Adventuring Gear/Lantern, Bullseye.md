*Adventuring Gear, Mundane*

A Bullseye Lantern is an oil lantern with a focused shutter that casts light in a directed beam.

***Value.*** 10 GP
***Weight.*** 2 lb.

A Bullseye Lantern burns [[Oil]] as fuel to cast [[Vision|Bright Light]] in a 60-foot [[Areas of Effect|Cone]] and [[Vision|Dim Light]] for an additional 60 feet.
