*Adventuring Gear, Mundane*

A Grappling Hook is a multi-pronged metal hook used with rope to catch ledges and railings.

***Value.*** 2 GP
***Weight.*** 4 lb.

As a [[Utilise|Utilise action]], you can throw the Grappling Hook at a railing, a ledge, or another catch within 50 feet of yourself, and the hook catches on if you succeed on a DC 13 [[Dexterity|Dexterity (Acrobatics)]] check. If you tied a [[Rope]] to the hook, you can then climb it.
