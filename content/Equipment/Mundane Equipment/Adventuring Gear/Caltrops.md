*Adventuring Gear, Mundane*

Caltrops are small, sharp metal spikes scattered across the ground to hinder movement.

***Value.*** 1 GP
***Weight.*** 2 lb.

As a [[Utilise|Utilise action]], you can spread Caltrops from their bag to cover a 5-foot Square area within 5 feet of yourself. A creature that enters this area for the first time on a turn must succeed on a DC 15 [[Saving Throws|Dexterity saving throw]] or take 1 Piercing damage and have its Speed reduced to 0 until the start of its next turn. It takes 10 minutes to recover the Caltrops.
