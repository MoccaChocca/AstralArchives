*Adventuring Gear, Mundane*

***Value.*** 5 SP
***Weight.*** 3 pounds

A Blanket is a warm woven covering used for rest, shelter, and protection against cold weather.

While wrapped in a Blanket, you have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on [[saving throws]] against extreme cold.
