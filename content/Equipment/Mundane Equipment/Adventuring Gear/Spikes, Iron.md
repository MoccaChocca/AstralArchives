*Adventuring Gear, Mundane*

Iron Spikes are sturdy metal wedges sold in bundles of ten.

***Value.*** 1 GP
***Weight.*** 5 lb.

Iron Spikes come in bundles of ten. As a [[Utilise]], you can use a blunt object, such as a [[Light Hammer]], to hammer a spike into wood, earth, or a similar material. You can do so to jam a door shut or to then tie a [[Rope]] or [[Chain]] to the Spike.
