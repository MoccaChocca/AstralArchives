*Adventuring Gear, Mundane*

A Lamp is a small oil-burning vessel that provides steady light.

***Value.*** 5 SP
***Weight.*** 1 lb.

A Lamp burns [[Oil]] as fuel to cast [[Vision|Bright Light]] in a 15-foot radius and [[Vision|Dim Light]] for an additional 30 feet.
