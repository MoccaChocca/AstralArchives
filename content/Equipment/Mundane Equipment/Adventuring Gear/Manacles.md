*Adventuring Gear, Mundane*

Manacles are metal restraints with locks used to bind a creature’s wrists or limbs.

***Value.*** 2 GP
***Weight.*** 6 lb.

As a [[Utilise|Utilise action]], you can use Manacles to bind an unwilling Small or Medium creature within 5 feet of yourself that has the [[Grappled]], [[Incapacitated]], or [[Restrained]] condition if you succeed on a DC 13 [[Dexterity|Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)]] check. While bound, a creature has [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on [[attack rolls]], and the creature is [[Restrained]] if the Manacles are attached to a chain or hook that is fixed in place. Escaping the Manacles requires a successful DC 20 [[Dexterity|Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)]] check as an [[action]]. Bursting them requires a successful DC 25 [[Strength|Strength (Athletics)]] check as an [[action]].

Each set of Manacles comes with a key. Without the key, a creature can use [[Thieves' Tools]] to pick the Manacles’ lock with a successful DC 15 [[Dexterity|Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)]] check.
