*Adventuring Gear, Mundane*

A typical Chain is a length of interlocked metal links used for binding, hauling, or securing objects.

***Value.*** 5 GP
***Weight.*** 10 lb.

As a [[Utilise|Utilise action]], you can wrap a Chain around an unwilling creature within 5 feet of yourself that has the [[Grappled]], [[Incapacitated]], or [[Restrained]] condition if you succeed on a DC 13 [[Strength|Strength (Athletics)]] check. If the creature’s legs are bound, the creature has the [[Restrained]] condition until it escapes. Escaping the Chain requires the creature to make a successful DC 18 [[Dexterity|Dexterity (Acrobatics)]] check as an [[action]]. Bursting the Chain requires a successful DC 20 [[Strength|Strength (Athletics)]] check as an [[action]].
