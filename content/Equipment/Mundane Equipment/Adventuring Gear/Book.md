*Adventuring Gear, Mundane*

A typical Book is a bound collection of written pages containing fiction, lore, or practical instruction.

***Value.*** 25 GP
***Weight.*** 5 lb.

A Book contains fiction or nonfiction. If you consult an accurate nonfiction Book about its topic, you gain a +5 bonus to [[Intelligence|Intelligence (Arcana, History, Nature, or Religion)]] checks you make about that topic.
