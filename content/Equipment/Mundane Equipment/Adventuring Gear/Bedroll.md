*Adventuring Gear, Mundane*

***Value.*** 1 GP
***Weight.*** 7 pounds

A Bedroll is a bundled sleeping mat and blanket used to rest outdoors or on hard ground.

A Bedroll sleeps one Small or Medium creature. While in a Bedroll, you automatically succeed on [[saving throws]] against extreme cold.
