*Adventuring Gear, Consumable, Mundane*

Perfume is a scented liquid kept in a small vial for social occasions.

***Value.*** 5 GP
***Weight.*** -

Perfume comes in a 4-ounce vial. For 1 hour after applying Perfume to yourself, you have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on [[Charisma|Charisma (Persuasion)]] checks made to [[influence]] an [[Attitude|Indifferent]] Humanoid within 5 feet of yourself.
