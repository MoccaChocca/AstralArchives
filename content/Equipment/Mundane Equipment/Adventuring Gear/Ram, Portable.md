*Adventuring Gear, Mundane*

A Portable Ram is a reinforced beam with handles used to batter doors and barriers.

***Value.*** 4 GP
***Weight.*** 35 lb.

You can use a Portable Ram to break down doors. When doing so, you gain a +4 bonus to the Strength check. One other character can help you use the ram, giving you [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on this check.
