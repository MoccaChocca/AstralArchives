*Adventuring Gear, Mundane*

A Block and Tackle is a set of pulleys and rope used to lift heavy loads.

***Value.*** 1 GP
***Weight.*** 5 lb.

A Block and Tackle allows you to hoist up to four times the weight you can normally lift.
