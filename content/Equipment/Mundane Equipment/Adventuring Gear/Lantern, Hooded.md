*Adventuring Gear, Mundane*

A Hooded Lantern is an oil lantern with a movable hood to dim or reveal its light.

***Value.*** 5 GP
***Weight.*** 2 lb.

A Hooded Lantern burns [[Oil]] as fuel to cast [[Vision|Bright Light]] in a 30-foot radius and [[Vision|Dim Light]] for an additional 30 feet. As a [[Bonus Action]], you can lower the hood, reducing the light to [[Vision|Dim Light]] in a 5-foot radius, or raise it again.
