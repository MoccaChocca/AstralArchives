*Adventuring Gear, Mundane*

A Pole is a straight 10-foot length of wood used for probing, vaulting, and reaching.

***Value.*** 5 CP
***Weight.*** 7 lb.

A Pole is 10 feet long. You can use it to touch something up to 10 feet away. If you must make a [[Strength|Strength (Athletics)]] check as part of a [[Rules/Core/Movement/Movement|High Jump or Long Jump]], you can use the Pole to vault, giving yourself [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on the check.
