*Adventuring Gear, Consumable, Mundane*

A typical vial of Antitoxin contains a sharp-smelling medicinal mixture used to resist poison.

***Value.*** 50 GP
***Weight.*** -

As a [[Bonus Action]], you can drink a vial of Antitoxin to gain [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on [[saving throws]] to avoid or end the [[Poisoned]] condition for 1 hour.
