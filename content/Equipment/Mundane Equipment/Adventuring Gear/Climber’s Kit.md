*Adventuring Gear, Mundane*

***Value.*** 25 GP
***Weight.*** 12 lb.

A Climber’s Kit includes boot tips, gloves, pitons, and a harness. As a [[Utilise|Utilise action]], you can use the Climber’s Kit to anchor yourself; when you do, you can’t fall more than 25 feet from the anchor point, and you can’t move more than 25 feet from there without undoing the anchor as a [[Bonus Action]].
