*Adventuring Gear, Mundane*

A Lock is a keyed metal fastening used to secure doors, chests, or containers.

***Value.*** 10 GP
***Weight.*** 1 lb.

A Lock comes with a key. Without the key, a creature can use [[Thieves' Tools]] to pick this Lock with a successful DC 15 [[Dexterity|Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)]] check.
