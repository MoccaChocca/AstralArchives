*Adventuring Gear, Consumable, Mundane*

A Healer’s Kit is a case of bandages, salves, and splints used to provide emergency care.

***Value.*** 5 GP
***Weight.*** 3 lb.

A Healer’s Kit has ten uses. As a [[Utilise|Utilise action]], you can expend one of its uses to stabilize an [[Unconscious]] creature that has 0 [[Hit Points]] without needing to make a [[Wisdom|Wisdom (Medicine)]] check.
