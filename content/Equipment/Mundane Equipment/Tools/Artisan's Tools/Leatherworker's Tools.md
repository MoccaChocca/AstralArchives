*Artisan’s Tools, Mundane*

Leatherworker’s Tools are specialised implements used to pursue a trade, make craftwork, and perform precise practical tasks.

***Value.*** 5 GP
***Weight.*** 5 lb.
***Ability.*** [[Dexterity]]

***Utilise.*** When you take the [[Utilise]] action with Leatherworker’s Tools, you can do one of the following:
- Add a design to a leather item (DC 10)

***Craft.*** [[Sling]], [[Whip]], [[Hide Armour]], [[Leather Armour]], [[Studded Leather Armour]], [[Backpack]], [[Crossbow Bolt Case]], [[Map or Scroll Case]], [[Parchment]], [[Pouch]], [[Quiver]], [[Waterskin]].
