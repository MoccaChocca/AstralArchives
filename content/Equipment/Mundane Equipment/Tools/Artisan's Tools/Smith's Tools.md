*Artisan’s Tools, Mundane*

Smith’s Tools are specialised implements used to pursue a trade, make craftwork, and perform precise practical tasks.

***Value.*** 20 GP
***Weight.*** 8 lb.
***Ability.*** [[Strength]]

***Utilise.*** When you take the [[Utilise]] action with Smith’s Tools, you can do one of the following:
- Pry open a door or container (DC 20)

***Craft.*** Any [[Melee Weapons|Melee weapon]] except [[Club]], [[Greatclub]], [[Quarterstaff]], and [[Whip]], Medium [[Armour]] except [[Hide Armour]], Heavy [[Armour]], [[Ball Bearings]], [[Bucket]], [[Caltrops]], [[Chain]], [[Crowbar]], [[Firearm Bullets]], [[Grappling Hook]], [[Iron Pot]], [[Iron Spikes]], [[Sling Bullets]].
