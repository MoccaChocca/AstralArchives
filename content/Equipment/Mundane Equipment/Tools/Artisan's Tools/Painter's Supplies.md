*Artisan’s Tools, Mundane*

Painter’s Supplies are specialised implements used to pursue a trade, make craftwork, and perform precise practical tasks.

***Value.*** 10 GP
***Weight.*** 5 lb.
***Ability.*** [[Wisdom]]

***Utilise.*** When you take the [[Utilise]] action with Painter’s Supplies, you can do one of the following:
- Paint a recognisable image of something you’ve seen (DC 10)

***Craft.*** [[Druidic Focus]], [[Holy Symbol]].
