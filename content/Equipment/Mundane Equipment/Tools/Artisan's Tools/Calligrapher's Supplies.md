*Artisan’s Tools, Mundane*

Calligrapher’s Supplies are specialised implements used to pursue a trade, make craftwork, and perform precise practical tasks.

***Value.*** 10 GP
***Weight.*** 5 lb.
***Ability.*** [[Dexterity]]

***Utilise.*** When you take the [[Utilise]] action with Calligrapher’s Supplies, you can do one of the following:
- Write text with impressive flourishes that guard against forgery (DC 15)

***Craft.*** [[Ink]], [[Spell Scrolls]].
