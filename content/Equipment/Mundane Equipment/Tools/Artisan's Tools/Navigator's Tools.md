*Other Tools, Mundane*

A Navigator’s Tools is a practical tool used for specialised adventuring tasks.

***Value.*** 25 GP
***Weight.*** 2 lb.
***Ability.*** [[Wisdom]]

***Utilise.*** When you take the [[Utilise]] action with Navigator’s Tools, you can do one of the following:
- Plot a course (DC 10)
- Determine position by stargazing (DC 15)
