*Other Tools, Mundane*

A Herbalism Kit is a practical tool used for specialised adventuring tasks.

***Value.*** 5 GP
***Weight.*** 3 lb.
***Ability.*** [[Intelligence]]

***Utilise.*** When you take the [[Utilise]] action with Herbalism Kit, you can do one of the following:
- Identify a plant (DC 10)

***Craft.*** [[Antitoxin]], [[Candle]], [[Healer's Kit]], [[Potion of Healing]].
