*Other Tools, Mundane*

A Forgery Kit is a practical tool used for specialised adventuring tasks.

***Value.*** 15 GP
***Weight.*** 5 lb.
***Ability.*** [[Dexterity]]

***Utilise.*** When you take the [[Utilise]] action with Forgery Kit, you can do one of the following:
- Mimic 10 or fewer words of someone else’s handwriting (DC 15)
- Duplicate a wax seal (DC 20)
