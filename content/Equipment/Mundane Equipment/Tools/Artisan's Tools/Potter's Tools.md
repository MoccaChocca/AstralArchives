*Artisan’s Tools, Mundane*

Potter’s Tools are specialised implements used to pursue a trade, make craftwork, and perform precise practical tasks.

***Value.*** 10 GP
***Weight.*** 3 lb.
***Ability.*** [[Intelligence]]

***Utilise.*** When you take the [[Utilise]] action with Potter’s Tools, you can do one of the following:
- Discern what a ceramic object held in the past 24 hours (DC 15)

***Craft.*** [[Jug]], [[Lamp]].
