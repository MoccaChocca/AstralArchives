*Artisan’s Tools, Mundane*

Brewer’s Supplies are specialised implements used to pursue a trade, make craftwork, and perform precise practical tasks.

***Value.*** 20 GP
***Weight.*** 9 lb.
***Ability.*** [[Intelligence]]

***Utilise.*** When you take the [[Utilise]] action with Brewer’s Supplies, you can do one of the following:
- Detect poisoned drink (DC 15)
- Identify alcohol (DC 10)

***Craft.*** [[Antitoxin]].
