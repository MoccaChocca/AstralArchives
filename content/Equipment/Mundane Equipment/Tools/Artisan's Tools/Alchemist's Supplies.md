*Artisan’s Tools, Mundane*

Alchemist’s Supplies are specialised implements used to pursue a trade, make craftwork, and perform precise practical tasks.

***Value.*** 50 GP
***Weight.*** 8 lb.
***Ability.*** [[Intelligence]]

***Utilise.*** When you take the [[Utilise]] action with Alchemist’s Supplies, you can do one of the following:
- Identify a substance (DC 15)
- Start a fire (DC 15)

***Craft.*** [[Acid]], [[Alchemist’s Fire]], [[Component Pouch]], [[Oil]], [[Paper]], [[Perfume]].
