*Artisan’s Tools, Mundane*

Carpenter’s Tools are specialised implements used to pursue a trade, make craftwork, and perform precise practical tasks.

***Value.*** 8 GP
***Weight.*** 6 lb.
***Ability.*** [[Strength]]

***Utilise.*** When you take the [[Utilise]] action with Carpenter’s Tools, you can do one of the following:
- Seal or pry open a door or container (DC 20)

***Craft.*** [[Club]], [[Greatclub]], [[Quarterstaff]], [[Barrel]], [[Chest]], [[Ladder]], [[Pole]], [[Portable Ram]], [[Torch]].
