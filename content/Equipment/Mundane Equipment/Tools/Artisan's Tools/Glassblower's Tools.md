*Artisan’s Tools, Mundane*

Glassblower’s Tools are specialised implements used to pursue a trade, make craftwork, and perform precise practical tasks.

***Value.*** 30 GP
***Weight.*** 5 lb.
***Ability.*** [[Intelligence]]

***Utilise.*** When you take the [[Utilise]] action with Glassblower’s Tools, you can do one of the following:
- Discern what a glass object held in the past 24 hours (DC 15)

***Craft.*** [[Glass Bottle]], [[Magnifying Glass]], [[Spyglass]], [[Vial]].
