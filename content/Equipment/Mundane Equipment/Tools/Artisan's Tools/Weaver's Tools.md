*Artisan’s Tools, Mundane*

Weaver’s Tools are specialised implements used to pursue a trade, make craftwork, and perform precise practical tasks.

***Value.*** 1 GP
***Weight.*** 5 lb.
***Ability.*** [[Dexterity]]

***Utilise.*** When you take the [[Utilise]] action with Weaver’s Tools, you can do one of the following:
- Mend a tear in clothing (DC 10)
- Sew a Tiny design (DC 10)

***Craft.*** [[Padded Armour]], [[Basket]], [[Bedroll]], [[Blanket]], [[Fine Clothes]], [[Net]], [[Robe]], [[Rope]], [[Sack]], [[String]], [[Tent]], [[Traveller’s Clothes]].
