*Artisan’s Tools, Mundane*

Tinker’s Tools are specialised implements used to pursue a trade, make craftwork, and perform precise practical tasks.

***Value.*** 50 GP
***Weight.*** 10 lb.
***Ability.*** [[Dexterity]]

***Utilise.*** When you take the [[Utilise]] action with Tinker’s Tools, you can do one of the following:
- Assemble a Tiny item composed of scrap, which falls apart in 1 minute (DC 20)

***Craft.*** [[Musket]], [[Pistol]], [[Bell]], [[Bullseye Lantern]], [[Flask]], [[Hooded Lantern]], [[Hunting Trap]], [[Lock]], [[Manacles]], [[Mirror]], [[Shovel]], [[Signal Whistle]], [[Tinderbox]].
