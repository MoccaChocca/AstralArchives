*Artisan’s Tools, Mundane*

Cobbler’s Tools are specialised implements used to pursue a trade, make craftwork, and perform precise practical tasks.

***Value.*** 5 GP
***Weight.*** 5 lb.
***Ability.*** [[Dexterity]]

***Utilise.*** When you take the [[Utilise]] [[action]] with Cobbler’s Tools, you can do one of the following:
- Modify footwear to give [[Advantage and Disadvantage|advantage]] on the wearer’s next [[Dexterity|Dexterity (Acrobatics)]] check (DC 10)

***Craft.*** [[Climber’s Kit]].
