*Artisan’s Tools, Mundane*

Cook’s Utensils are specialised implements used to pursue a trade, make craftwork, and perform precise practical tasks.

***Value.*** 1 GP
***Weight.*** 8 lb.
***Ability.*** [[Wisdom]]

***Utilise.*** When you take the [[Utilise]] action with Cook’s Utensils, you can do one of the following:
- Improve food’s flavour (DC 10)
- Detect spoiled or poisoned food (DC 15)

***Craft.*** [[Rations]].
