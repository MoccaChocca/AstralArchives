*Artisan’s Tools, Mundane*

Woodcarver’s Tools are specialised implements used to pursue a trade, make craftwork, and perform precise practical tasks.

***Value.*** 1 GP
***Weight.*** 5 lb.
***Ability.*** [[Dexterity]]

***Utilise.*** When you take the [[Utilise]] action with Woodcarver’s Tools, you can do one of the following:
- Carve a pattern in wood (DC 10)

***Craft.*** [[Club]], [[Greatclub]], [[Quarterstaff]], [[Ranged Weapons]] except [[Pistol]], [[Musket]], and [[Sling]], [[Arcane Focus]], [[Arrows]], [[Bolts]], [[Druidic Focus]], [[Ink Pen]], [[Needles]].
