*Other Tools, Mundane*

A Thieves’ Tools is a practical tool used for specialised adventuring tasks.

***Value.*** 25 GP
***Weight.*** 1 lb.
***Ability.*** [[Dexterity]]

***Utilise.*** When you take the [[Utilise]] action with Thieves’ Tools, you can do one of the following:
- Pick a lock (DC 15)
- Disarm a trap (DC 15)
