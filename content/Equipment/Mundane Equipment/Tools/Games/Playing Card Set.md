*Gaming Set, Mundane*

A Playing Card Set contains the pieces or cards needed to play its game of chance or strategy.

***Value.*** 5 SP
***Weight.*** —
***Ability.*** [[Wisdom]]

***Utilise.*** When you take the [[Utilise]] action with Playing Card Set, you can do one of the following:
- Discern whether someone is cheating (DC 10)
- Win the game (DC 20)
