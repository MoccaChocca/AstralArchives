*Musical Instrument, Mundane*

A Drum is a musical instrument used to perform practised tunes or improvised songs.

***Value.*** 6 GP
***Weight.*** 3 lb.
***Ability.*** [[Charisma]]

***Utilise.*** When you take the [[Utilise]] action with Drum, you can do one of the following:
- Play a known tune (DC 10)
- Improvise a song (DC 15)
