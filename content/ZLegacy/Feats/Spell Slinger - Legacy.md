_Prerequisite: The ability to cast at least one spell_

You have learned techniques to cast ranged attack spells with precision and control, even while engaged in close combat, gaining the following benefits:
- Being within 5ft of a hostile creature doesn’t impose [[Advantage and Disadvantage|disadvantage]] on your ranged spell attacks
- Your ranged spell [[attack rolls]] ignore [[Cover|half cover]] and [[Cover|three-quarters cover]]
- You learn one cantrip that requires an attack roll. Choose the cantrip from the bard, cleric, druid, sorcerer, warlock, or wizard spell list. Your spellcasting ability for this cantrip depends on the spell list you chose from: Charisma for bard, sorcerer, or warlock; Wisdom for cleric or druid; or Intelligence for wizard.
