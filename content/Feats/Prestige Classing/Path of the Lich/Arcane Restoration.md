*Prerequisite: [[Lich Initiate]] feat*

The souls you consume fuel your magic, replenishing your spellcasting with stolen vitality.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Essence Rejuvenation.*** When you use Soul Rejuvenation to consume a soul, you may recover one or more spell slots. The spell slots can have a combined level equal to your [[proficiency bonus]], up to a maximum of 5th level. Once you use this feature you cannot do so again until you finish a [[Resting|short or long rest]].
