*Prerequisite: [[Lich Initiate]] feat*
  
The souls you consume leave behind a residue of stolen vitality, which you can shape into a ward against death for yourself or another creature.  
  
***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
  
***Soul Transference.*** When you use Soul Siphon to consume a soul, you can choose a creature within 60 feet of yourself to gain [[Temporary Hit Points]] equal to your [[Proficiency Bonus]] plus your spellcasting ability modifier (minimum of 1 [[Temporary Hit Points|Temporary Hit Point]]).

***Soul Shield.*** At the start of your turn, if you have one of the following conditions, you can expend 1 Soul Point to end that condition on yourself: [[Charmed]], [[Frightened]] or [[Stunned]].
