*General Feat (Prerequisite: [[Mark of Warding]] Feat)*

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Improved Intuition.*** When you use the Warder’s Intuition benefit of your [[Mark of Warding]] feat, you can roll 1d6 instead of 1d4.

***Improved Warding.*** When a creature makes an [[Attack Rolls|attack roll]] against you or a creature you can see within 30 feet of yourself, you can take a [[Reaction]] to impose Disadvantage on that roll. You can use this benefit a number of times equal to your [[Proficiency Bonus]], and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [[Resting|Long Rest]].
