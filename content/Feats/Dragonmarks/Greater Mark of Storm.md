*General Feat (Prerequisite: [[Mark of Storm]] Feat)*

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Improved Intuition.*** When you use the Windwright’s Intuition benefit of your [[Mark of Storm]] feat, you can roll 1d6 instead of 1d4.

***Improved Storm.*** When you use the Storm Magic benefit of your [[Mark of Storm]] feat to cast *[[Gust of Wind]]* without a spell slot, you also gain a [[Rules/Core/Movement/Movement|Fly Speed]] of 60 feet for the [[Duration|duration]] of the spell.
