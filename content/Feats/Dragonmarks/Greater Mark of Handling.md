*General Feat (Prerequisite: [[Mark of Handling]] Feat)*

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Improved Intuition.*** When you use the Wild Intuition benefit of your [[Mark of Handling]] feat, you can roll 1d6 instead of 1d4.

***Improved Handling.*** While you are mounted, immediately after you hit a target within 5 feet of your mount with a melee [[Attack Rolls|attack roll]], your mount can take a [[Reaction]] to move up to its Speed or take the [[Attack Action|Attack action]] to make one attack only (your choice).

***Subdue Animal.*** As a [[Magic Action|Magic action]], you exert command over one Beast or Monstrosity you can see within 30 feet of yourself. The target must succeed on a [[Saving Throws|Wisdom saving throw]] (DC 8 plus your [[Wisdom]] modifier and [[Proficiency Bonus]]) or have the [[Frightened]] condition until the start of your next turn. You can use this benefit a number of times equal to your [[Proficiency Bonus]], and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [[Resting|Long Rest]].
