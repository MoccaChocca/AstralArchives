*General Feat (Prerequisite: [[Mark of Finding]] Feat)*

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Improved Intuition.*** When you use the Hunter’s Intuition benefit of your [[Mark of Finding]] feat, you can roll 1d6 instead of 1d4.

***Improved Finding.*** When you use the Finder’s Magic benefit of your [[Mark of Finding]] to cast *[[Hunter's Mark]]* without a spell slot, the range of the spell is doubled, and you can modify the spell so that the target can’t benefit from the [[Invisible]] condition for the [[Duration|duration]].
