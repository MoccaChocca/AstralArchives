You gain the following benefits.

***Thieves’ Cant.*** You know [[Thieves' Cant|Thieves’ Cant]].

***Instrument Training.*** You gain proficiency with a Musical Instrument of your choice.

***Distracting Melody.*** When you take the [[Help|Help action]] to assist an ally’s attack roll, the enemy you’re distracting can be within 30 feet of you, rather than within 5 feet of you, provided the enemy can see or hear you.
