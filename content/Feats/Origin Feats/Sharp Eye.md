When you take the [[Search|Search]] or [[Study|Study]] action, you can give yourself advantage on any ability check made as part of that action. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your [[Proficiency Bonus]], and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [[Resting|Long Rest]]. If the check fails, the use of this feature isn’t expended.
