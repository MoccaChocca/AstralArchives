You gain the following benefits.

***Inspiring Strike.*** Once per turn when you score a [[Critical Hits|Critical Hit]] against a creature, you can choose an ally within 30 feet of yourself who can see or hear you and who lacks [[Heroic Inspiration]]. That ally gains Heroic Inspiration.

***Reassert Honour.*** When an enemy you can see deals damage to an ally of yours that is within 5 feet of you, you have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|advantage]] on your next [[Attack Rolls|attack roll]] against that enemy before the end of your next turn.
