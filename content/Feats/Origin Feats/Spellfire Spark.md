You gain the following benefits.

***Magic Absorption.*** Once per turn, when you take damage from a spell or magical effect, you reduce the total damage taken by 1d4. You can’t use this benefit if you have the [[Incapacitated]] condition.

***Spellfire Flame.*** You learn the _[[Sacred Flame]]_ cantrip. [[Intelligence]], [[Wisdom]], or [[Charisma]] is your spellcasting ability for this spell (choose when you select this feat). You can also cast this cantrip as a [[Bonus Action]] a number of times equal to your [[Proficiency Bonus]], and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [[Resting|Long Rest]].
