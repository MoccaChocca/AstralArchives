*Planar Pact Feat (Prerequisite: Can’t Have Another Planar Pact Feat)*

You gain the following benefits.

***Infernal Resistance.*** You have [[Damage Resistance and Vulnerability|Resistance]] to Fire damage and Poison damage.

***Infernal Sight.*** You can see normally in [[Vision|Dim Light]] and [[Vision|Darkness]] - both magical and nonmagical - within 60 feet of yourself.

***Silver-Tongued.*** You gain proficiency in the Deception skill.
