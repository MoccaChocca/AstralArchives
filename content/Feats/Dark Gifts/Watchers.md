*Dark Gift Feat*

Something unnatural is always watching you, taking the form of scurrying vermin and other eerie creatures. You gain the following features.

***Borrowed Eyes.*** You always have the *[[Beast Sense]]* and *[[Speak with Animals]]* spells prepared. You can cast each spell without a spell slot, and you must finish a [[Resting|Long Rest]] before you can cast it in this way again. You can also cast these spells using spell slots you have of the appropriate level.

***Heightened Suspicion.*** Whenever you take the [[Search|Search]] action, you can roll 1d4 and add the number rolled to any ability check made as part of that action.

***Incessant Watchers.*** You have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on [[Saving Throws|saving throws]] made against the *[[Scrying]]* spell.

In addition, immediately after you make a [[D20 Tests|D20 Test]] and roll a 1 on the d20, paranoia threatens to overwhelm you. Make a [[Saving Throws|Wisdom saving throw]] (DC 13 plus your [[Proficiency Bonus]]). On a failed save, you have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on [[D20 Tests]] for 1 minute. You can repeat the save at the end of each of your turns, ending the effect early on a success.
