*Dark Gift Feat*

Deathly power resides within you, bursting out at the slightest provocation. You gain the following features.

***Death Touch.*** You learn the *[[Chill Touch]]* spell and can cast it without [[Components|spell components]]. Necrotic damage you deal with this spell ignores [[Resistance]]. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this spell (choose when you select this feat).

***Pull of the Grave.*** You have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on [[Death Saving Throws]].
