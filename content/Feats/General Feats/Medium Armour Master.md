*Prerequisite: Medium Armour Training*

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength or Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Dexterous Wearer.*** While you're wearing Medium Armour, you can add 3, rather than 2, to your AC if you have a [[Dexterity]] score of 16 or higher.
