***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Constitution score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Defy Death.*** You have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on [[Death Saving Throws]].

***Speedy Recovery.*** As a [[Bonus Action]], you can expend one of your [[Hit Points|Hit Point Dice]], roll the die, and regain a number of [[Hit Points]] equal to the roll.
