*Prerequisite: Dexterity 13+*

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Blindsight.*** You have [[Special Senses|Blindsight]] with a range of 10 feet.

***Fog of War.*** You exploit the distractions of battle, gaining [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on any [[Dexterity|Dexterity (Stealth)]] check you make as part of the [[Hide|Hide action]] during combat.

***Sniper.*** If you make an [[Attack Rolls|attack roll]] while [[hidden]] and the roll misses, making the [[Attack Rolls|attack roll]] doesn’t reveal your location.
