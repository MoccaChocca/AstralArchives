*Prerequisite: Shield proficiency*

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Shield Bash.*** If you attack a creature within 5 feet of you as part of the [[Attack action]] and hit with a Melee weapon, you can immediately bash the target with your [[Equipment/Mundane Equipment/Armour/Shield|Shield]] if it's equipped, forcing the target to make a [[Saving Throws|Strength saving throw]] (DC 8 plus your [[Strength]] modifier and [[Proficiency Bonus]]). On a failed save, you either push the target 5 feet from you or cause it to have the [[Prone]] condition (your choice). You can use this benefit only once on each of your turns.

***Interpose Shield.*** If you're subjected to an effect that allows you to make a [[Saving Throws|Dexterity Saving Throw]] to take only half damage, you can take a [[Reaction]] to take no damage if you succeed on the [[Saving Throws|saving throw]] and are holding a [[Equipment/Mundane Equipment/Armour/Shield|Shield]].
