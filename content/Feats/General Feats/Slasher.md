***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength or Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Hamstring.*** Once per turn when you hit a creature with an attack that deals Slashing damage, you can reduce the [[Rules/Combat/Movement/Movement|Speed]] of that creature by 10 feet until the start of your next turn.

***Enhanced Critical.*** When you score a [[Critical Hits|Critical Hit]] that deals Slashing damage to a creature, it has [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on [[attack rolls]] until the start of your next turn.
