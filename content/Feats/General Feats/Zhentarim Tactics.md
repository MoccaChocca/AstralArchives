*Prerequisite: [[Zhentarim Ruffian]] Feat*

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Dexterity or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Retaliate.*** Immediately after a creature within 5 feet of you hits you with a melee attack, you can make an [[Opportunity Attacks|Opportunity Attack]] against that creature.

***Versatile Merc.*** When you finish a [[Resting|Long Rest]], choose a skill in which you have proficiency. You have [[Expertise]] in that skill until you finish your next [[Resting|Long Rest]].
