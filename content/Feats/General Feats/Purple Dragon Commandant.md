*Prerequisite: [[Purple Dragon Rook]] Feat or Martial Weapon Proficiency*

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength or Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Encourage Ally.*** As a [[Bonus Action]], you bolster one ally you can see within 30 feet. The ally gains [[Temporary Hit Points]] equal to 2d6 plus the modifier of the ability score increased by this feat. You can take this [[Bonus Action]] a number of times equal to your [[Proficiency Bonus]], and you regain all uses when you finish a [[Resting|Long Rest]].

***Last Stand.*** You have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on [[attack rolls]] while [[Bloodied]].
