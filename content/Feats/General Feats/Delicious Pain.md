***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Toughened Flesh.*** Immediately after you take Bludgeoning, Piercing, or Slashing damage, you can take a [[Reaction]] to gain [[Resistance]] to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage until the start of your next turn. Once you use this benefit, you can’t use it again until you finish a [[Resting|Short or Long Rest]].
