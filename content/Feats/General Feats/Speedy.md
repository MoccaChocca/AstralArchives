*Prerequisite: Dexterity or Constitution 13+*

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Dexterity or Constitution score by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
  
***Speed Increase.*** Your [[Rules/Combat/Movement/Movement|Speed]] increases by 10 feet.  
  
***Dash over Difficult Terrain.*** When you take the [[Dash|Dash action]], [[Difficult Terrain]] doesn’t cost you extra [[Rules/Combat/Movement/Movement|movement]] for the rest of the current turn.  
  
***Agile Movement.*** Opportunity Attacks against you have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]].
