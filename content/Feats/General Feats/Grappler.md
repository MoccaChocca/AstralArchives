*Prerequisite: Strength or Dexterity 13+*

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength or Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Punch and Grab.*** When you hit a creature with an Unarmed Strike as part of the [[Attack action]] on your turn, you can use both the damage and the grapple option. You can use this benefit only once per turn.

***Attack Advantage.*** You have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on [[attack rolls]] against a creature [[Grappled]] by you.

***Fast Wrestler.*** You don’t have to spend extra [[Rules/Combat/Movement/Movement|movement]] to move a creature [[Grappled]] by you if the creature is your size or smaller.
