*Prerequisite: [[Lords' Alliance Agent]] Feat*

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Standard Bearer.*** As a [[Bonus Action]], choose up to three creatures within 60 feet of yourself that can see you. Each target can immediately take a [[Reaction]] to right itself and end the [[Prone]] condition, provided its [[Rules/Combat/Movement/Movement|Speed]] isn’t 0.

Additionally, you bolster the targets’ resolve, which lasts for 1 minute or until you have the [[Incapacitated]] condition. While bolstered, a target can’t be possessed or gain the [[Charmed]] or [[Frightened]] condition; if a target is already possessed, [[Charmed]], or [[Frightened]], the target has [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on any new [[Saving Throws|saving throw]] against the relevant effect.

Once you use this benefit, you can’t do so again until you finish a [[Resting|Long Rest]].
