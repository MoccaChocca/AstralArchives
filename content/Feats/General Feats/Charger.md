*Prerequisite: Strength or Dexterity 13+*

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength or Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Improved Dash.*** When you take the [[Dash|Dash action]], your Speed increases by 10 feet for that [[action]].

***Charge Attack.*** Once per turn, if you move at least 10 feet in a straight line toward a target immediately before hitting it with a melee attack, you can either deal an extra 1d8 damage to the target or push it up to 10 feet away from you if it is no more than one size larger than you.
