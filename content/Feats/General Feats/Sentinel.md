*Prerequisite: Strength or Dexterity 13+*

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength or Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Guardian.*** Immediately after a creature within 5 feet of you takes the [[Disengage|Disengage action]] or hits a target other than you with an attack, you can make an [[Opportunity Attacks|Opportunity Attack]] against that creature.

***Halt.*** When you hit a creature with an [[Opportunity Attacks|Opportunity Attack]], the creature’s [[Rules/Combat/Movement/Movement|Speed]] becomes 0 for the rest of the current turn.
