*Prerequisite: Dexterity 13+*

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Ignore Loading.*** You ignore the [[Loading]] property of crossbows.

***Close-Quarters Shooting.*** Being within 5 feet of an enemy doesn’t impose [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on your [[Attack Rolls|attack rolls]] with crossbows.

***Dual Wielding.*** When you make the extra attack granted by the Light property, you can add your ability modifier to the damage of the extra attack if that attack is made with a crossbow that has the Light property.
