*Prerequisite: [[Tyro of the Gauntlet]] Feat*

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength, Wisdom, or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Resurge.*** When you have the [[Prone]] condition, you can right yourself with only 5 feet of [[Rules/Combat/Movement/Movement|movement]].

***Stronger Together.*** If you are within 5 feet of an ally that doesn’t have the [[Incapacitated]] condition, you and that ally have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on [[Saving Throws|Strength saving throws]]. You can’t use this benefit while you have the [[Incapacitated]] condition.
