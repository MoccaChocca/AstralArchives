*Prerequisite: Strength or Dexterity 13+*

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength or Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

***Climb Speed.*** You gain a [[Movement|Climb Speed]] equal to your Speed.

***Quick Rise.*** When you have the [[Prone]] condition, you can stand up by spending only 5 feet of [[Movement|movement]].

***Running Jump.*** You need to move only 5 feet before making a [[Movement|Long Jump]] or [[Movement|High Jump]], rather than the normal 10 feet.
