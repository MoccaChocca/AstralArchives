*Epic Boon Feat (Prerequisite: Level 19+)*

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 30.

***Peerless Aim.*** When you miss with an [[Attack Rolls|attack roll]], you can hit instead. Once you use this benefit, you can’t use it again until the start of your next turn.
