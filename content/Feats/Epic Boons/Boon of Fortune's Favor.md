*Epic Boon Feat (Prerequisite: Level 19+)*

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 30.

***Saving Throw Reroll.*** When you fail a [[Saving Throws|saving throw]], you can reroll it and must use the new roll. Once you use this benefit, you can’t do so again until the start of your next turn.
