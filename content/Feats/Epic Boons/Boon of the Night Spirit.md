*Epic Boon Feat (Prerequisite: Level 19+)*

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 30.

***Merge with Shadows.*** While within [[Vision|Dim Light]] or [[Vision|Darkness]], you can give yourself the [[Invisible]] condition as a [[Bonus Action]]. The condition ends on you immediately after you take an action, a [[Bonus Action]], or a [[Reaction]].

***Shadowy Form.*** While within [[Vision|Dim Light]] or [[Vision|Darkness]], you have [[Resistance]] to all damage except Psychic and Radiant.
