*Epic Boon Feat (Prerequisite: Level 19+)*

You gain the following benefits.

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 30.

***Shadowy Stretch.*** When you take the [[Attack Action|Attack action]], your reach for your Melee weapon attacks increases by 10 feet until the end of your turn.

***Dancing Silhouette.*** You can take the [[Dodge|Dodge action]] as a [[Bonus Action]].
