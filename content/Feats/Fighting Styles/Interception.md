*Prerequisite: Fighting Style Feature*

When a creature you can see hits another creature within 5 feet of you with an [[Attack Rolls|attack roll]], you can take a [[Reaction]] to reduce the damage dealt to the target by 1d10 plus your [[Proficiency Bonus]]. You must be holding a [[Equipment/Mundane Equipment/Armour/Shield|Shield]] or a Simple or Martial weapon to use this [[Reaction]].
