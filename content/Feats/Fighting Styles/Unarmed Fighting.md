*Prerequisite: Fighting Style Feature*

When you hit with your Unarmed Strike and deal damage, you can deal Bludgeoning damage equal to 1d6 plus your [[Strength]] modifier instead of the normal damage of an Unarmed Strike. If you aren't holding any weapons or a [[Equipment/Mundane Equipment/Armour/Shield|Shield]] when you make the [[Attack Rolls|attack roll]], the d6 becomes a d8.

At the start of each of your turns, you can deal 1d4 Bludgeoning damage to one creature [[Grappled]] by you.
