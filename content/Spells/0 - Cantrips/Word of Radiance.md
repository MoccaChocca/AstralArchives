_Evocation Cantrip (Cleric)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** Self  
**Components:** V, M (a sunburst token)  
**Duration:** Instantaneous

Burning radiance erupts from you in a 5-foot [[Areas of Effect|Emanation]]. Each creature of your choice that you can see in it must succeed on a [[Saving Throws|Constitution saving throw]] or take 1d6 Radiant damage.

**Cantrip Upgrade.** The damage increases by 1d6 when you reach levels 5 (2d6), 11 (3d6), and 17 (4d6).
