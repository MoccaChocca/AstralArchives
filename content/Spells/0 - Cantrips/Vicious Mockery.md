_Enchantment Cantrip (Bard)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You unleash a string of insults laced with subtle enchantments at one creature you can see or hear within range. The target must succeed on a [[Saving Throws|Wisdom saving throw]] or take 1d6 Psychic damage and have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on the next attack roll it makes before the end of its next turn.

***Cantrip Upgrade.*** The damage increases by 1d6 when you reach levels 5 (2d6), 11 (3d6), and 17 (4d6).
