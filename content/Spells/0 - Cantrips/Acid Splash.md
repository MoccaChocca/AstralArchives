_Evocation Cantrip (Artificer, Sorcerer, Wizard)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You create an acidic bubble at a point within range, where it explodes in a 5-foot-radius [[Areas of Effect|Sphere]]. Each creature in that Sphere must succeed on a [[Saving Throws|Dexterity saving throw]] or take 1d6 Acid damage.

***Cantrip Upgrade.*** The damage increases by 1d6 when you reach levels 5 (2d6), 11 (3d6), and 17 (4d6).
