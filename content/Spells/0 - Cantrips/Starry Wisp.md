_Evocation Cantrip (Bard, Druid)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** 60 feet  
**Components:** V, S  
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You launch a mote of light at one creature or object within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d8 Radiant damage, and until the end of your next turn, it emits [[Vision|Dim Light]] in a 10-foot radius and can’t benefit from the [[Invisible]] condition.

***Cantrip Upgrade.*** The damage increases by 1d8 when you reach levels 5 (2d8), 11 (3d8), and 17 (4d8).
