_Level 7 Evocation (Cleric, Druid, Sorcerer)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** 150 feet  
**Components:** V, S  
**Duration:** Instantaneous

A storm of fire appears within range. The area of the storm consists of up to ten 10-foot [[Areas of Effect|Cubes]], which you arrange as you like. Each Cube must be contiguous with at least one other Cube. Each creature in the area makes a [[Saving Throws|Dexterity saving throw]], taking 7d10 Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

Flammable objects in the area that aren’t being worn or carried start burning.
