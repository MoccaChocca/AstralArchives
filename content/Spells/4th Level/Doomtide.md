_Level 4 Conjuration (Bard, Cleric, Warlock)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]
**Range:** 120 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (soot and a dried eel)
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

You create a 20-foot-radius [[Areas of Effect|Sphere]] of inky fog within range. The fog is [[Vision|magical Darkness]] and lasts for the duration or until a strong wind (such as the one created by the *[[Gust of Wind|Gust of Wind]]* spell) disperses it, ending the spell.

Each creature in the Sphere when it appears makes a [[Saving Throws|Wisdom saving throw]]. On a failed save, a creature takes 5d6 Psychic damage and subtracts 1d6 from its [[saving throws]] until the end of its next turn. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage only. A creature also makes this save when the Sphere moves into its space, when it enters the Sphere, or when it ends its turn inside the Sphere. A creature makes this save only once per turn.

The Sphere moves 10 feet away from you at the start of each of your turns.
