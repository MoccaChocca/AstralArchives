_Level 4 Abjuration (Artificer, Bard, Cleric, Druid, Ranger)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** Touch  
**Components:** V, S, M (a leather strap)  
**Duration:** 1 hour

You touch a willing creature. For the duration, the target’s [[Rules/Combat/Movement/Movement|movement]] is unaffected by [[Difficult Terrain]], and spells and other magical effects can neither reduce the target’s Speed nor cause the target to have the [[Paralysed]] or [[Restrained]] conditions. The target also has a [[Rules/Core/Movement/Movement|Swim Speed]] equal to its Speed.

In addition, the target can spend 5 feet of [[Rules/Combat/Movement/Movement|movement]] to automatically escape from nonmagical restraints, such as manacles or a creature imposing the [[Grappled]] condition on it.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 4.
