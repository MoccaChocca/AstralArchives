_Level 4 Evocation (Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** Self  
**Components:** V, S, M (a bit of phosphorus or a firefly)  
**Duration:** 10 minutes

Wispy flames wreathe your body for the duration, shedding [[Vision|Bright Light]] in a 10-foot radius and [[Vision|Dim Light]] for an additional 10 feet.

The flames provide you with a warm shield or a chill shield, as you choose. The warm shield grants you [[Damage Resistance and Vulnerability|Resistance]] to Cold damage, and the chill shield grants you [[Damage Resistance and Vulnerability|Resistance]] to Fire damage.

In addition, whenever a creature within 5 feet of you hits you with a melee [[Attack Rolls|attack roll]], the shield erupts with flame. The attacker takes 2d8 Fire damage from a warm shield or 2d8 Cold damage from a chill shield.
