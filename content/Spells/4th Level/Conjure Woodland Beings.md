_Level 4 Conjuration (Druid, Ranger)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** Self  
**Components:** V, S  
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

You conjure nature spirits that flit around you in a 10-foot [[Areas of Effect|Emanation]] for the duration. Whenever the Emanation enters the space of a creature you can see and whenever a creature you can see enters the Emanation or ends its turn there, you can force that creature to make a [[Saving Throws|Wisdom saving throw]]. The creature takes 5d8 Force damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature makes this save only once per turn.

In addition, you can take the [[Disengage|Disengage action]] as a [[Bonus Action]] for the spell’s duration.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 4.
