_Level 4 Abjuration (Cleric, Paladin)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** Self  
**Components:** V  
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

An aura radiates from you in a 30-foot [[Areas of Effect|Emanation]] for the duration. While in the aura, you and your allies have [[Damage Resistance and Vulnerability|Resistance]] to Necrotic damage, and your [[hit points|Hit Point maximums]] can’t be reduced. If an ally with 0 [[Hit Points]] starts its turn in the aura, that ally regains 1 [[Hit Points|Hit Point]].
