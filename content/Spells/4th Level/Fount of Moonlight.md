_Level 4 Evocation (Bard, Druid)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** Self  
**Components:** V, S  
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

A cool light wreathes your body for the duration, emitting [[Vision|Bright Light]] in a 20-foot radius and [[Vision|Dim Light]] for an additional 20 feet.

Until the spell ends, you have [[Damage Resistance and Vulnerability|Resistance]] to Radiant damage, and your melee attacks deal an extra 2d6 Radiant damage on a hit.

In addition, immediately after you take damage from a creature you can see within 60 feet of yourself, you can take a [[Reaction]] to force the creature to make a [[Saving Throws|Constitution saving throw]]. On a failed save, the creature has the [[Blinded]] condition until the end of your next turn.
