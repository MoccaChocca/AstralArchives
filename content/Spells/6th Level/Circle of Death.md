_Level 6 Necromancy (Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** 150 feet  
**Components:** V, S, M (the powder of a crushed black pearl worth 500+ GP)  
**Duration:** Instantaneous

Negative energy ripples out in a 60-foot-radius [[Areas of Effect|Sphere]] from a point you choose within range. Each creature in that area makes a [[Saving Throws|Constitution saving throw]], taking 8d8 Necrotic damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 2d8 for each spell slot level above 6.
