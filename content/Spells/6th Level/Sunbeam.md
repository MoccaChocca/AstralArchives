_Level 6 Evocation (Cleric, Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** Self  
**Components:** V, S, M (a magnifying glass)  
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

You launch a sunbeam in a 5-foot-wide, 60-foot-long [[Areas of Effect|Line]]. Each creature in the Line makes a [[Saving Throws|Constitution saving throw]]. On a failed save, a creature takes 6d8 Radiant damage and has the [[Blinded]] condition until the start of your next turn. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage only.

Until the spell ends, you can take a [[Magic action]] to create a new [[Areas of Effect|Line]] of radiance.

For the [[duration]], a mote of brilliant radiance shines above you. It sheds [[Vision|Bright Light]] in a 30-foot radius and [[Vision|Dim Light]] for an additional 30 feet. This light is sunlight.
