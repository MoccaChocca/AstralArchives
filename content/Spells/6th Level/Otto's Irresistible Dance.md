_Level 6 Enchantment (Bard, Wizard)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Components:** V  
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

One creature that you can see within range must make a [[Saving Throws|Wisdom saving throw]]. On a successful save, the target dances comically until the end of its next turn, during which it must spend all its [[Rules/Combat/Movement/Movement|movement]] to dance in place.

On a failed save, the target has the [[Charmed]] condition for the [[duration]]. While [[Charmed]], the target dances comically, must use all its [[Rules/Combat/Movement/Movement|movement]] to dance in place, and has [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on [[Saving Throws|Dexterity saving throws]] and [[attack rolls]], and other creatures have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on [[attack rolls]] against it. On each of its turns, the target can take an [[action]] to collect itself and repeat the save, ending the spell on itself on a success.
