_Level 6 Necromancy (Cleric)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** 60 feet  
**Components:** V, S  
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You unleash virulent magic on a creature you can see within range. The target makes a [[Saving Throws|Constitution saving throw]]. On a failed save, it takes 14d6 Necrotic damage, and its [[Hit Points|Hit Point maximum]] is reduced by an amount equal to the Necrotic damage it took. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage only. This spell can’t reduce a target’s [[Hit Points|Hit Point maximum]] below 1.
