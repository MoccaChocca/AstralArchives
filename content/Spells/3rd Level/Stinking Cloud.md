_Level 3 Conjuration (Bard, Sorcerer, Wizard)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** 90 feet  
**Components:** V, S, M (a rotten egg)  
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

You create a 20-foot-radius [[Areas of Effect|Sphere]] of yellow, nauseating gas centered on a point within range. The cloud is [[Vision|Heavily Obscured]]. The cloud lingers in the air for the duration or until a strong wind (such as the one created by *[[Gust of Wind]]*) disperses it.

Each creature that starts its turn in the Sphere must succeed on a [[Saving Throws|Constitution saving throw]] or have the [[Poisoned]] condition until the end of the current turn. While [[Poisoned]] in this way, the creature can’t take an [[action]] or a [[Bonus Action]].
