_Level 3 Abjuration (Cleric)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]
**Range:** 30 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

Choose any number of creatures within range. For the duration, each target has [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on [[Saving Throws|Wisdom saving throws]] and [[Death Saving Throws]] and regains the maximum number of [[Hit Points]] possible from any healing.
