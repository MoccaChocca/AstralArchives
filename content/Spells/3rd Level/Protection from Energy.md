_Level 3 Abjuration (Artificer, Cleric, Druid, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 hour

For the duration, the willing creature you touch has [[Damage Resistance and Vulnerability|Resistance]] to one damage type of your choice: Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Thunder.
