_Level 3 Abjuration (Cleric, Druid, Paladin)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** Self  
**Components:** V  
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

An aura radiates from you in a 30-foot [[Areas of Effect|Emanation]] for the duration. When you create the aura and at the start of each of your turns while it persists, you can restore 2d6 [[Hit Points]] to one creature in it.
