_Level 3 Evocation (Paladin)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** Self  
**Components:** V  
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

You radiate a magical aura in a 30-foot [[Areas of Effect|Emanation]]. While in the aura, you and your allies each deal an extra 1d4 Radiant damage when hitting with a weapon or an Unarmed Strike.
