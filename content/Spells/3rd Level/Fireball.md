_Level 3 Evocation (Sorcerer, Wizard)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]
**Range:** 150 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (a ball of bat guano and sulphur)
**Duration:** Instantaneous

A bright streak flashes from you to a point you choose within range and then blossoms with a low roar into a fiery explosion. Each creature in a 20-foot-radius [[Areas of Effect|Sphere]] centred on that point makes a [[Saving Throws|Dexterity saving throw]], taking 8d6 Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

Flammable objects in the area that aren’t being worn or carried start burning.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 3.
