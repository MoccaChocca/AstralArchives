_Level 3 Transmutation (Bard, Druid, Ranger)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]] (Overgrowth) or 8 hours (Enrichment)  
**Range:** 150 feet  
**Components:** V, S  
**Duration:** Instantaneous

This spell channels vitality into plants. The casting time you use determines whether the spell has the Overgrowth or the Enrichment effect below.

***Overgrowth.*** Choose a point within range. All normal plants in a 100-foot-radius [[Areas of Effect|Sphere]] centered on that point become thick and overgrown. A creature moving through that area must spend 4 feet of [[Rules/Combat/Movement/Movement|movement]] for every 1 foot it moves. You can exclude one or more areas of any size within the spell’s area from being affected.

***Enrichment.*** All plants in a half-mile radius centred on a point within range become enriched for 365 days. The plants yield twice the normal amount of [[Food and Water|food]] when harvested. They can benefit from only one Plant Growth per year.
