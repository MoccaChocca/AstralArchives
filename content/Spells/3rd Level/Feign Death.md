_Level 3 Necromancy (Bard, Cleric, Druid, Wizard)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]] or [[Rituals|Ritual]]  
**Range:** Touch  
**Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of graveyard dirt)  
**Duration:** 1 hour

You touch a willing creature and put it into a cataleptic state that is indistinguishable from death.

For the duration, the target appears dead to outward inspection and to spells used to determine the target’s status. The target has the [[Blinded]] and [[Incapacitated]] conditions, and its Speed is 0.

The target also has [[Damage Resistance and Vulnerability|Resistance]] to all damage except Psychic damage, and it has [[Immunity]] to the [[Poisoned]] condition.
