_Level 3 Illusion (Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S, M (a white feather)
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

Each creature in a 30-foot [[Areas of Effect|Cone]] must succeed on a [[Saving Throws|Wisdom saving throw]] or drop whatever it is holding and have the [[Frightened]] condition for the duration.

A [[Frightened]] creature takes the [[Dash|Dash action]] and moves away from you by the safest route on each of its turns unless there is nowhere to move. If the creature ends its turn in a space where it doesn’t have line of sight to you, the creature makes a [[Saving Throws|Wisdom saving throw]]. On a successful save, the spell ends on that creature.
