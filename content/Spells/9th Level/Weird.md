_Level 9 Illusion (Warlock, Wizard)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** 120 feet  
**Components:** V, S  
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

You try to create illusory terrors in others' minds. Each creature of your choice in a 30-foot-radius [[Areas of Effect|Sphere]] centered on a point within range makes a [[Saving Throws|Wisdom saving throw]]. On a failed save, a target takes 10d10 Psychic damage and has the [[Frightened]] condition for the duration. On a successful save, a target takes half as much damage only.

A [[Frightened]] target makes a [[Saving Throws|Wisdom saving throw]] at the end of each of its turns. On a failed save, it takes 5d10 Psychic damage. On a successful save, the spell ends on that target.
