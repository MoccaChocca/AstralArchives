_Level 9 Divination (Bard, Druid, Warlock, Wizard)_

**Casting Time:** 1 minute  
**Range:** Touch  
**Components:** V, S, M (a hummingbird feather)  
**Duration:** 8 hours

You touch a willing creature and bestow a limited ability to see into the immediate future. For the duration, the target has [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on [[D20 Tests]], and other creatures have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on [[attack rolls]] against it. The spell ends early if you cast it again.
