_Level 8 Abjuration (Cleric)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** Self  
**Components:** V, S, M (a reliquary worth 1,000+ GP)  
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

For the duration, you emit an aura in a 30-foot [[Areas of Effect|Emanation]]. While in the aura, creatures of your choice have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on all [[saving throws]], and other creatures have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on [[attack rolls]] against them. In addition, when a Fiend or an Undead hits an affected creature with a melee attack roll, the attacker must succeed on a [[Saving Throws|Constitution saving throw]] or have the [[Blinded]] condition until the end of its next turn.
