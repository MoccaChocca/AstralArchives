_Level 8 Evocation (Cleric, Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** 150 feet  
**Components:** V, S, M (a piece of sunstone)  
**Duration:** Instantaneous

Brilliant sunlight flashes in a 60-foot-radius [[Areas of Effect|Sphere]] centred on a point you choose within range. Each creature in the Sphere makes a [[Saving Throws|Constitution saving throw]]. On a failed save, a creature takes 12d6 Radiant damage and has the [[Blinded]] condition for 1 minute. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage only.

A creature [[Blinded]] by this spell makes another [[Saving Throws|Constitution saving throw]] at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

This spell dispels [[Vision|Darkness]] in its area that was created by any spell.
