_Level 2 Necromancy (Sorcerer, Wizard)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** Touch  
**Components:** V, S, M (an onyx worth 50+ GP, which the spell consumes)  
**Duration:** 1 hour

For the duration, an inky aura surrounds one creature you touch. The target has [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on [[Death Saving Throws]], and once per turn, when a creature within 5 feet of the target hits it with a melee [[Attack Rolls|attack roll]], the attacker takes 2d4 Necrotic damage.
