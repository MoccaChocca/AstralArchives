_Level 2 Abjuration (Artificer, Cleric, Druid, Paladin, Ranger)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** Touch  
**Components:** V, S  
**Duration:** 1 hour

You touch a creature and end the [[Poisoned]] condition on it. For the duration, the target has [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on [[saving throws]] to avoid or end the [[Poisoned]] condition, and it has [[Damage Resistance and Vulnerability|Resistance]] to Poison damage.
