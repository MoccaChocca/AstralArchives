_Level 2 Illusion (Artificer, Sorcerer, Wizard)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** Self  
**Components:** V  
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

Your body becomes blurred. For the duration, any creature has [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on [[attack rolls]] against you. An attacker is immune to this effect if it perceives you with [[Special Senses|Blindsight]] or [[Special Senses|Truesight]].
