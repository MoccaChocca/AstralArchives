_Level 2 Abjuration (Wizard)_

**Casting Time:** [[Bonus Action]]
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

Arcane wards protect you against magic for the duration. You have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on [[saving throws]] against spells and magical effects. Additionally, if you succeed on a [[Saving Throws|saving throw]] against a spell or magical effect and would normally take half as much damage, you instead take no damage.
