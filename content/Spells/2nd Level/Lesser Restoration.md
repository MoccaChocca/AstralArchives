_Level 2 Abjuration (Artificer, Bard, Cleric, Druid, Paladin, Ranger)_

**Casting Time:** [[Bonus Action]]
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You touch a creature and end one condition on it: [[Blinded]], [[Deafened]], [[Paralysed]], or [[Poisoned]].
