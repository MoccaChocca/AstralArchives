_Level 2 Evocation (Artificer, Cleric, Druid, Wizard)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** Touch  
**Components:** V, S, M (ruby dust worth 50+ GP, which the spell consumes)  
**Duration:** Until dispelled

A flame springs from an object that you touch. The effect casts [[Vision|Bright Light]] in a 20-foot radius and [[Vision|Dim Light]] for an additional 20 feet. It looks like a regular flame, but it creates no heat and consumes no fuel. The flame can be covered or hidden but not smothered or quenched.
