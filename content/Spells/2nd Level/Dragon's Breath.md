_Level 2 Transmutation (Artificer, Sorcerer, Wizard)_

**Casting Time:** [[Bonus Action]]  
**Range:** Touch  
**Components:** V, S, M (a hot pepper)  
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

You touch one willing creature, and choose Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Poison. Until the spell ends, the target can take a Magic action to exhale a 15-foot Cone. Each creature in that area makes a [[Saving Throws|Dexterity saving throw]], taking 3d6 damage of the chosen type on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 2.
