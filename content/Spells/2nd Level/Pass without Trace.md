_Level 2 Abjuration (Druid, Ranger)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** Self  
**Components:** V, S, M (ashes from burned mistletoe)  
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 hour

You radiate a concealing aura in a 30-foot Emanation for the duration. While in the aura, you and each creature you choose have a +10 bonus to [[Dexterity|Dexterity (Stealth)]] checks and leave no tracks.
