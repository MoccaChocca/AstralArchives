_Level 5 Evocation (Cleric)_

**Casting Time:** Action  
**Range:** 60 feet  
**Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of sulfur)  
**Duration:** Instantaneous

A vertical column of brilliant fire roars down from above. Each creature in a 10-foot-radius, 40-foot-high [[Areas of Effect|Cylinder]] centered on a point within range makes a [[Saving Throws|Dexterity saving throw]], taking 5d6 Fire damage and 5d6 Radiant damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The Fire damage and the Radiant damage increase by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 5.
