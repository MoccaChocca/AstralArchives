_Level 5 Conjuration (Ranger)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** 150 feet  
**Components:** V, S, M (a Melee or Ranged weapon worth at least 1 CP)  
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You brandish the weapon used to cast the spell and choose a point within range. Hundreds of similar spectral weapons (or ammunition appropriate to the weapon) fall in a volley and then disappear. Each creature of your choice that you can see in a 40-foot-radius, 20-foot-high [[Areas of Effect|Cylinder]] centred on that point makes a [[Saving Throws|Dexterity saving throw]]. A creature takes 8d8 Force damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.
