_Level 5 Transmutation (Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** Self  
**Components:** V, S, M (a pearl worth 100+ GP)  
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

You imbue yourself with the elemental power of genies. You gain the following benefits until the spell ends:

***Elemental Immunity.*** When you cast this spell, choose one of the following damage types: Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Thunder. You have [[Damage Resistance and Vulnerability|Resistance]] to the chosen damage type.

***Elemental Pulse.*** When you cast this spell and at the start of each of your subsequent turns, you release a burst of elemental energy in a 15-foot [[Areas of Effect|Emanation]] originating from yourself. Each creature of your choice in that area makes a [[Saving Throws|Dexterity saving throw]]. On a failed save, a creature takes 2d6 Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Thunder damage (your choice) and has the [[Prone]] condition. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage only.

***Flight.*** You gain a [[Fly]] Speed of 30 feet and can hover.
