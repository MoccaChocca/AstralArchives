_Level 5 Abjuration (Artificer, Cleric, Paladin, Wizard)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** Self  
**Components:** V  
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

An aura radiates from you in a 30-foot [[Areas of Effect|Emanation]] for the [[duration]]. While in the aura, you and your allies have [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on [[saving throws]] against spells and other magical effects. When an affected creature makes a [[Saving Throws|saving throw]] against a spell or magical effect that allows a save to take only half damage, it takes no damage if it succeeds on the save.
