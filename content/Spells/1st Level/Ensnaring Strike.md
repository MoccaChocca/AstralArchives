_Level 1 Conjuration (Ranger)_

**Casting Time:** [[Bonus Action]], which you take immediately after hitting a creature with a weapon  
**Range:** Self  
**Components:** V  
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

As you hit the target, grasping vines appear on it, and it makes a [[Saving Throws|Strength saving throw]]. A Large or larger creature has [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on this save. On a failed save, the target has the [[Restrained]] condition until the spell ends. On a successful save, the vines shrivel away, and the spell ends.

While [[Restrained]], the target takes 1d6 Piercing damage at the start of each of its turns. The target or a creature within reach of it can take an [[action]] to make a [[Strength|Strength (Athletics)]] check against your spell save DC. On a success, the spell ends.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 1.
