_Level 1 Enchantment (Bard, Druid, Ranger)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Components:** V, S, M (a morsel of food)  
**Duration:** 24 hours

Target a Beast that you can see within range. The target must succeed on a [[Saving Throws|Wisdom saving throw]] or have the [[Charmed]] condition for the duration. If you or one of your allies deals damage to the target, the spell ends.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** You can target one additional Beast for each spell slot level above 1.
