_Level 1 Enchantment (Bard, Cleric, Warlock)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Components:** V, S, M (a drop of blood)  
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

Up to three creatures of your choice that you can see within range must each make a [[Saving Throws|Charisma saving throw]]. Whenever a target that fails this save makes an attack roll or a [[Saving Throws|saving throw]] before the spell ends, the target must subtract 1d4 from the attack roll or save.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 1.
