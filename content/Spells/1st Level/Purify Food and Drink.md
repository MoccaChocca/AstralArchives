_Level 1 Transmutation (Artificer, Cleric, Druid, Paladin)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]] or [[Rituals|Ritual]]  
**Range:** 10 feet  
**Components:** V, S  
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You remove poison and rot from nonmagical [[Food and Water|food and drink]] in a 5-foot-radius [[Areas of Effect|Sphere]] centred on a point within range.
