_Level 1 Evocation (Paladin)_

**Casting Time:** [[Bonus Action]], which you take immediately after hitting a target with a Melee Weapon or an Unarmed Strike  
**Range:** Self  
**Components:** V  
**Duration:** 1 minute

As you hit the target, it takes an extra 1d6 Fire damage from the attack and 1d6 [[Persistent Damage|Persistent Fire Damage]]. At the end of each of its turns until the spell ends, the target makes a [[Saving Throws|Constitution saving throw]]. On a successful save, the [[Persistent Damage]] ends.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** All the damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 1.
