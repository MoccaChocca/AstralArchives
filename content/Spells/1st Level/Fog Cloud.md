_Level 1 Conjuration (Druid, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** 120 feet  
**Components:** V, S  
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 hour

You create a 20-foot-radius [[Areas of Effect|Sphere]] of fog centred on a point within range. The Sphere is [[Vision|Heavily Obscured]]. It lasts for the duration or until a strong wind (such as one created by *[[Gust of Wind]]*) disperses it.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The fog’s radius increases by 20 feet for each spell slot level above 1.
