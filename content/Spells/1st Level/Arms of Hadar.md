_Level 1 Conjuration (Warlock)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** Self  
**Components:** V, S  
**Duration:** Instantaneous

Invoking Hadar, you cause tendrils to erupt from yourself. Each creature in a 10-foot [[Areas of Effect|Emanation]] originating from you makes a [[Saving Throws|Strength saving throw]]. On a failed save, a target takes 2d6 Necrotic damage and can’t take Reactions until the start of its next turn. On a successful save, a target takes half as much damage only.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 1.
