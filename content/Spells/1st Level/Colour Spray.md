_Level 1 Illusion (Bard, Sorcerer, Wizard)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** Self  
**Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of colourful sand)  
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You launch a dazzling array of flashing, colourful light. Each creature in a 15-foot [[Areas of Effect|Cone]] originating from you must succeed on a [[Saving Throws|Constitution saving throw]] or have the [[Blinded]] condition until the end of your next turn.
