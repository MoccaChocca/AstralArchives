_Level 1 Evocation (Cleric)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** 120 feet  
**Components:** V, S  
**Duration:** 1 round

You hurl a bolt of light toward a creature within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, it takes 4d6 Radiant damage, and the next [[Attack Rolls|attack roll]] made against it before the end of your next turn has [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]].

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 1.
