_Level 1 Enchantment (Bard)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** 120 feet.  
**Components:** V, S  
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

One creature of your choice that you can see within range hears a haunting yet catchy tune that continuously repeats in its mind. The target must succeed on a [[Saving Throws|Wisdom saving throw]], or it has [[Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on [[Intelligence]] checks and on [[Saving Throws|Constitution saving throws]] made to maintain [[Concentration]]. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the spell on itself on a success.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 1.
