_Level 1 Evocation (Sorcerer, Wizard)_

**Casting Time:** [[Action]]  
**Range:** Self  
**Components:** V, S  
**Duration:** Instantaneous

A thin sheet of flames shoots forth from you. Each creature in a 15-foot [[Areas of Effect|Cone]] makes a [[Saving Throws|Dexterity saving throw]], taking 3d6 Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

Flammable objects in the Cone that aren’t being worn or carried start burning.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 1.
