_Level 1 Abjuration (Artificer, Cleric)_

**Casting Time:** [[Bonus Action]]  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Components:** V, S, M (a shard of glass from a mirror)  
**Duration:** 1 minute

You ward a creature within range. Until the spell ends, any creature who targets the warded creature with an [[Attack Rolls|attack roll]] or a damaging spell must succeed on a [[Saving Throws|Wisdom saving throw]] or either choose a new target or lose the attack or spell. This spell doesn’t protect the warded creature from [[areas of effect]].

The spell ends if the warded creature makes an [[Attack Rolls|attack roll]], casts a spell, or deals damage.
